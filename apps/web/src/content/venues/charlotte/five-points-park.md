Five Points Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park at 200 French
Street, in Biddleville, a few blocks west of Johnson C. Smith University. The
county's parks ordinance governs dogs here, and Section 26 runs one sentence:
"It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are
expressly prohibited." Dogs are allowed by default. Only a posted sign narrows
that, and the county has no page for this park — the facility slug returns a
404 — so nothing specific to Five Points appears online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds. Water is the same story. No drinking fountain
and no restroom are mapped inside the property, and nothing published mentions
either one.

A stream crosses the northwest corner of the park, running toward culverted
segments outside the boundary. Section 11 of the parks ordinance covers that
water directly: no bathing dogs "in the streams, lakes, or other water
features of any park."

The built parts sit close together. Two tennis courts, a paved basketball
court with two hoops, playground equipment in two separate sections, four
benches, and concrete walks linking them. There are two parking areas, one of
them a small asphalt surface lot. French Street runs along the near edge, and
the rest of the property is open ground under mature trees.

The county's own park record leaves the size field blank, so there is no
official acreage. Directories give it as nine acres and as 9.8. The closing
time is posted at the park and published nowhere online, and (980) 314-1002,
the number in the county record, rings the Central Region parks office rather
than anything on site.

Frazier Park is about a mile south, off West Fourth Street Extension. Anita
Stroud Park sits roughly a mile and a quarter east on Double Oaks Road.
