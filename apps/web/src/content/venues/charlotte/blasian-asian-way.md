Blasian Asian Way is a shipping-container stall on the Keswick Avenue
pedestrian walkway at Camp North End, in the row of food stalls that opens onto
a shared open-air dining area. Ordering happens at a window. There's no dining
room, so the usual indoor-or-patio question doesn't come up here.

The stall itself publishes nothing about dogs. Camp North End does. The campus
FAQ puts it in two sentences: "Yes! Camp North End is dog-friendly. Please keep
your furry friends on a leash and clean up after them." A second Camp North End
page tightens that to "most outdoor areas (except for the ford green turf)" and
notes that individual tenants set policy for their own indoor spaces. Blasian
Asian Way doesn't have one.

The Ford Green sits in the Boileryard district, a separate part of campus from
the Keswick stalls. Camp North End restates the exclusion for its movie nights:
"We ask that you do not allow your pet on the Ford Green, but you're welcome to
enjoy the areas surrounding the Ford Green."

Water bowls aren't mentioned by the stall or by the campus, one way or the
other.

Parking is unusually easy for the address. The lot at 1801 N. Graham St is
free, as is the surface lot at 1724 Statesville Ave; the garage at 120 Razades
Way is free for three hours, then $4 an hour. Public restrooms run throughout
the property.

The food is bulgogi in about six formats: rice bowls, loaded fries, tacos,
sandwiches, wings, fried dumplings. Galbi, chicken, a steak version they call
Seoulgogi, Cajun salmon, tofu. Mac and cheese appears Saturdays only. The
operation started as a food truck and still runs one, separate from the stall.

Hours are where the sources split. Camp North End's tenant directory lists
Sunday noon to 4, closed Monday, 11 to 7 Tuesday through Thursday, 11 to 8
Friday and Saturday. Blasian Asian Way's own site declines to publish any,
saying only that the weekly schedule goes up on social media.

Birdsong Brewing is a little over a mile southeast on North Davidson, and NoDa
Brewing's North End taproom is about a mile east.
