Devonshire Park is a Mecklenburg County park at 6330 Barrington Drive, in the
Devonshire neighborhood east of Albemarle Road. The county's parks ordinance is
the document that governs dogs here, and Section 26 runs one sentence: "It
shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are
expressly prohibited." Dogs are allowed by default. A posted sign is the only
thing that narrows that, and the county publishes no per-park rules page for
Devonshire, so nothing site-specific appears online either way.

The leash rule is published, and it is not park-specific. Charlotte-Mecklenburg
Animal Care & Control: "ALL dog owners that take their dogs for walks in their
neighborhoods and/or in public parks (not designated as a dog park) are
required to keep their dogs on leash and under physical restraint at ALL
TIMES." First violation runs $50.

The park is 1.7 acres, and it is new again. The county rebuilt it under a $7
million program covering ten existing neighborhood parks, and its project page
lists the official opening as June 4, 2024. Before that work it was a
playground and walking trails.

Almost everything built sits in one cluster at the east end: three playground
areas, a picnic shelter, seven benches, and concrete walks tying them together.
The fourteen mapped trees stand in that same corner. West of the cluster the
ground opens out with no canopy over it.

No parking lot is mapped on the property. Barrington Drive runs along the
front, and parking is on-street. Cochrane Middle School's track and ball fields
begin roughly 250 yards west, and Devonshire Elementary sits about 200 yards
south on the same street.

No drinking fountain is mapped inside the park and nothing published mentions
one. Restrooms are the same story. Closing time is posted on site and published
nowhere online.

Eastway Park is about a mile and a half west. Barkingham Park, the nearest
fenced off-leash area, is roughly two and a half miles northeast at Reedy Creek.
