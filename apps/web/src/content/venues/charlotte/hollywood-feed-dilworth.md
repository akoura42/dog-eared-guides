Hollywood Feed publishes the policy in the company's own words: "Customers are
encouraged to bring leashed dogs in the store, giving employees a chance to
engage and develop a friendship with both the animal and the customer." Dogs go
inside, on the sales floor.

One thing about where that sentence lives. It sits on Hollywood Feed's
corporate introduction site, written in first person by the company, on a page
aimed at landlords and property managers rather than shoppers. It's chain-wide.
No Charlotte location page repeats it, and neither does this store's Facebook
page.

Which store this is matters, because Charlotte has six. The company calls this
one Dilworth, on its own participating-locations list and in the name of the
Facebook page it runs for the address.

The self-wash is here. Dilworth appears under self wash on the chain's grooming
page, $15.99, no appointment. Shampoo, conditioner, towels, grooming brushes,
and blow dryers are provided, and staff handle the hair and dirt left behind.
The tubs, per the company, "feature doors, stairs, or ramps" — the thing a
visitor can confirm within a minute of walking to the back. Wash stations close
half an hour before the store does. Frequent Feeder counts washes, twelve for a
free thirteenth.

Grooming isn't at this location. Of the Charlotte stores, only Toringdon on
Copper Way is marked for on-staff groomers.

Store hours aren't readable on the location page, and the phone line above is
the store's direct number rather than the chain's 1-888 line.

Park Road runs south from uptown through Dilworth here. Freedom Park is under a
mile southeast, with the Little Sugar Creek Greenway running through it. 300
East is about a mile north on East Boulevard.
