Ninth Street Dog Park sits at 417 West Ninth Street, in Fourth Ward on the
western side of Uptown. It's a Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation
facility, listed on the county's dog parks page alongside Frazier, Davie, and
Barkingham. That page is where the rules come from, and they apply across the
county's dog parks rather than being written for this one.

The rules are short. County dog parks are "open daily from 7:30 a.m. to
sunset," which puts closing time on a sliding scale through the year: past
nine on a long June evening, mid-afternoon dark in December. Vaccinations are
a condition of entry. The county's language is "Rabies with Tag Number, DHLPP
& Bordetella," and it's written as the requirement for using the off-leash
areas. No children under the age of 12 are allowed. The county also says
owners "must follow all posted dog park rules," which makes the signage on
site the operative document once you're through the entrance.

That off-leash line is the county's only published statement about leashing
here. Everything past it is unpublished. Fencing, a divided area for small and
large dogs, water, shade, agility obstacles, and whether there's any fee:
third-party directories and apps describe several of these, but none of it
appears in the county's own rules, so this listing doesn't carry it. The
county's general information line is 980-314-1000.

Two naming details worth knowing before searching. The county writes it
"Ninth Street Dog Park"; OpenStreetMap, the Uptown business district site, and
most review platforms write it "9th Street Dog Park." And it's frequently
described as a First Ward park. The address puts it in Fourth Ward, west of
Tryon, several blocks from the LYNX 9th Street station that carries the same
number.
