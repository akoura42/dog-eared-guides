PetSmart's dog policy is published rather than reported, and it puts dogs on
the sales floor. The chain's In-Store Pet Policy lists domestic dogs among the
pets "permitted in PetSmart stores provided the pets are appropriately secured
(leashed or safely confined) and vaccinated (as appropriate for the particular
type of pet)." It applies to every location. This one is store #1510, the North
Lake Mall store, at 10245 Perimeter Parkway.

No leash length appears anywhere in the policy. Leashed or safely confined is
where it stops, which leaves Mecklenburg County's ordinance as the operative
rule: physical restraint anywhere off your own property. Water bowls go
unmentioned, on the policy page and the store page both. The policy also
reserves "the right to prohibit any pet from being in a PetSmart location for
any reason."

One rule here doesn't apply to shoppers. The store page lists Veterinary Care
and links it to Banfield, and the chain policy treats vet patients differently
from customers. Pets arriving for appointments "must be secured in a crate,
kennel, or other fastened enclosure to prevent exposure to the public," and may
not come out "until safely sheltered inside a hospital examination room or
kennel area." Walking a dog in on a leash is what the policy describes for
shopping, not for a vet visit.

Smaller format than the Cotswold store. Grooming, training, adoptions, curbside
pickup, and the vet. No PetsHotel and no Doggie Day Camp are listed here, so
overnight boarding and day care aren't part of this building.

Hours as posted: 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday, 10 to 7 on Sunday.
Grooming and training both run until 9. The vet hospital closes at 6, adoptions
at 7, and curbside pickup runs daily from open until 7.

The storefront faces Perimeter Parkway in the Northlake retail cluster, about
half a mile southwest of Northlake Mall. Clarks Creek Dog Park is roughly two
and a half miles east, off Hucks Road.
