NoDa Brewing's North End taproom sits at 150 W 32nd St, a block west of North
Tryon. It's the production brewery as well — tanks on one side, taproom and a
large outdoor beer garden on the other. The brewery's site currently lists this
location and a counter inside Concourse A at Charlotte Douglas, near Gate A29.
Older dog guides still point to a North Davidson taproom, which no longer
appears on the site.

NoDa publishes no year-round dog policy. Not on the taproom page, not in the
code of conduct, nowhere on nodabrewing.com. This listing rests on visitor
reports instead. A Charlotte dog trainer's 2025 roundup and a local dog
publication both place dogs in the outdoor spaces here, and a TripAdvisor
reviewer calls it dog friendly without saying where. Outdoor areas is the
claim those sources agree on. Indoor access is unconfirmed either way.

What the brewery does publish is event language. Several times a year the turf
gets fenced off into a free off-leash dog park run with the Humane Society of
Charlotte, most recently Rescue Brew Dog Park on August 7-9, 2026. That dog
park is set up in the overflow parking lot, up a flight of stairs from the main
lot. One event page states the rule directly: "All dogs must be leashed when
outside of the dog park." Another notes the taproom and beer garden operate
"as normal" during those weekends.

Water bowls, treats, size or breed limits, indoor access: none of it is
published by the brewery one way or the other. The tap list rotates and isn't
posted as a fixed menu. Posted hours run Tuesday through Sunday — 11:30 a.m. to
9 p.m. Tuesday through Thursday, to 10 p.m. Friday and Saturday, to 8 p.m.
Sunday. The phone line, 704-900-6851, is answered weekdays only.
