Bao & Broth is a stall on the first floor of Optimist Hall, the food hall at
1115 North Brevard Street in Optimist Park. The dog rule here belongs to the
building, not the stall. Optimist Hall's FAQ puts it in two sentences: "Your
furry friends are welcome to enjoy our outdoor spaces, including the Main
Courtyard. Due to health codes, however, any dog that is not a service animal
is not permitted inside the food hall."

The counter is inside. The courtyard is outside. Ordering ramen means someone
goes in while someone stays out.

That split is what the North Carolina food code contemplates. 15A NCAC 18A
.2656 allows dogs in outdoor dining areas as long as they're physically
restrained and don't pass through any indoor part of the establishment.
Optimist Hall's version of the rule matches it, and the hall states it in
writing rather than leaving it to a manager on shift.

Courtyard seating is first come, first serve, per the same FAQ. The hall
publishes nothing about leashes and nothing about water bowls one way or the
other, so Mecklenburg County's restraint ordinance is the rule that applies.

Hall hours as published: 7am to 9pm Monday through Thursday, 7am to 10pm
Friday, 8am to 10pm Saturday, 8am to 9pm Sunday. The FAQ notes individual
tenant hours vary, and Bao & Broth's own site is unreadable to our fetcher, so
the stall's specific closing time isn't confirmed here. Parking runs through a
registration program the hall describes as covering the first hour and a half
free.

One first-floor detail that's easy to spot on arrival: Pet Wants, a pet food
shop, is a tenant of the hall itself, listed alongside the food stalls on the
tenant directory.

Birdsong Brewing's taproom is about a quarter mile away on North Davidson,
where leashed dogs are welcome and a bowl comes on request.
