Double Oaks Park is Mecklenburg County land, which settles the dog question.
The county's Park and Recreation Facilities Ordinance covers pets in one
sentence, Sec. 26: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of County
parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Allowed by default, then, with
posted signs the only thing that narrows it. The county retired its per-park
pages, and the mecknc.gov address still attached to this park in OpenStreetMap
404s, so nothing published online names Double Oaks either way.

The county's own record calls the park undeveloped. Charlotte's parks GIS layer
carries it as a Neighborhood park, status "Undeveloped," in the Central
district. Brightwalk's developer site describes a passive park with public art
and informal fields for soccer, volleyball and touch football, written in the
future tense years ago. How much of that stands on the ground now isn't
published anywhere.

I-77 forms the western boundary. The Exit 12 ramps to La Salle Street and
Atando Avenue tie into the freeway at the park's northwest corner, so highway
noise carries across the whole parcel and doesn't let up. The rest of the
boundary is residential: Double Oaks Road below, Newland Road beyond that, the
rebuilt Brightwalk blocks on the other three sides.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule here. Nothing published mentions water, and no drinking fountain
is mapped on the property.

The county leaves the size field blank in its record, so there's no official
acreage; directories print 16.5 acres. Those same directories give the address
as 2605 Double Oaks Road, while the county record reads 2603. The number
(980) 314-1002 rings the Central Region parks office, not anything on site.

Anita Stroud Park sits about a third of a mile south on the same street, at
2215 Double Oaks Road. Double Oaks Family Aquatic Center, also county-run, is
roughly half a mile southeast at 2014 Statesville Avenue.
