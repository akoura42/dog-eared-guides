Anita Stroud Park is a six-acre Mecklenburg County neighborhood park at 2215
Double Oaks Road, sitting on the banks of Irwin Creek a short distance east of
Interstate 77. The county publishes no dog rule for this park by name. What it
publishes is systemwide, and it's consistent: Park and Recreation's own
guidance treats leashed dogs as welcome in the county's parks and greenways,
and CMPD Animal Care and Control states that dogs walked in public parks that
aren't designated dog parks stay leashed and under physical restraint at all
times. Six feet or shorter is the length the county names, in its greenway
rules.

Access is the thing to know first. The county calls this a walk-up facility,
and its own volunteer listing gives the parking instruction in one line: park
legally on Woodward Avenue, without blocking any driveways. No lot.

Inside the six acres are a half-court basketball court, two picnic shelters
with grills, two playground areas built for ages 2 to 5 and 5 to 12, and a
walking trail. The county describes the playgrounds as inclusive, and pointed
families here in July 2025 while Nevin Park's playground was closed for
construction. Whether pets are excluded from the playground surfacing isn't
stated on any readable county page. Nor is the trail's surface, its length, or
whether the park has a working water fountain.

The Irwin Creek Greenway now under construction is a different stretch of the
same creek. That segment runs upstream between Statesville Road and Allen Hills
Park, with substantial completion anticipated in spring 2026. It doesn't reach
Double Oaks Road.

For off-leash, the nearest county option is the fenced dog park at Frazier
Park, roughly a mile and a half south toward uptown, split into separate small
and large sections.
