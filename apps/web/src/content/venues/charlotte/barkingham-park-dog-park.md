Barkingham Park is Mecklenburg County's dog park inside Reedy Creek Park, off
Rocky River Road in northeast Charlotte. The county gives it a page of its own
under Park and Recreation's dog parks section. That page carries the name, a
map link, and nothing else. The rules live on the section index instead, and
they're written for the program rather than for this site.

They run short. County dog parks are "open daily from 7:30 a.m. to sunset," so
closing slides across the year: past nine on a long June evening, mid-afternoon
dark in December. Vaccinations are a condition of entry, in the county's
wording "Rabies with Tag Number, DHLPP & Bordetella," required "to utilize the
off-leash areas." No children under the age of 12 are allowed. Owners "must
follow all posted dog park rules," which makes the sign at the gate the
document that counts once you're through it.

Off-leash reaches the dog park and stops there. Charlotte-Mecklenburg Animal
Care and Control states that "ALL dog owners that take their dogs for walks in
their neighborhoods and/or in public parks (not designated as a dog park) are
required to keep their dogs on leash and under physical restraint at ALL
TIMES."

Reedy Creek Park holds more than this. The Laine Clontz Pavilion, the rentable
shelter at the same 2900 Rocky River Road address, publishes a line of its own:
"No pets allowed except for trained service animals." Reedy Creek Nature Center
and Preserve has a separate entrance and a separate address, 2986 Rocky River
Road, a short way further up the same road. Its page says nothing about dogs
either way. The preserve is posted as open daily, sunup to sundown.

What the county doesn't publish about Barkingham: acreage, fencing detail,
whether the run is divided for small and large dogs, water, a fee. Directories
describe four acres and a split. None of it appears in county material, so this
listing doesn't carry it.

Ninth Street Dog Park in Fourth Ward and the dog park at Frazier Park run under
the identical county rules.
