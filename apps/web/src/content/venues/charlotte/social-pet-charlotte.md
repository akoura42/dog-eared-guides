Social Pet's Charlotte location sits at 3814 Monroe Road, next door to Animal
Medical Hospital at 3832. Drop-off only. Dogs come here to stay for a day or a
week, and this location runs lodging, daycare, and grooming; training is listed
at the other two.

The door rule is vaccines, and the company prints the list: "Your dog needs to
be at least 4 months old and current on all vaccinations. We require Rabies,
Bordetella, Distemper, Parainfluenza, and a negative Fecal test." Canine
influenza is recommended, not required. Records get confirmed before a first
booking. Nothing on the site addresses spay/neuter status, and no breed or
weight limit appears anywhere.

The first visit is itself an evaluation. "Each dog participates in a
comprehensive assessment during their first day of daycare," with staff
watching body language and play style before assigning a group. Enrichment
playgroups cap at ten. Size and temperament decide the grouping, and one-on-one
sessions with a staff member run for dogs that don't want a group at all.

Water is one of the few amenities the FAQ states plainly: fresh water in every
play zone and in the accommodations, bowls run through a dishwasher daily.
Rawhides and rope toys aren't allowed in the building.

Hours are split, seven days a week. Seven to noon, then two to seven, which
means no midday pickup. Holidays can close daycare.

No staff member sleeps on site. Overnight coverage is "separate security and
fire monitoring systems with live, 24-7 monitoring and dispatch," with a team
member on call. Webcams are available to watch group play.

Money: daycare drops to $44 a day inside a package, and lodging starts at $70.
Per-visit daycare pricing isn't posted. Reservations run through Gingr, and the
phone number reaches a shared line for all three locations.

The Charlotte building has a sensory garden, which the other two locations
don't. Common Market Oakwold is about half a mile east on the same road.
