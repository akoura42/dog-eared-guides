Brawley's Beverage sits at 4620 Park Road in Madison Park, a bottle shop with a
taproom attached and outdoor seating alongside it. The place publishes no dog
policy. Its domain, brawleysbeverage.com, is a parked for-sale page rather than
a working site, and the Instagram account that functions as its live channel
has never posted a rule about dogs. This listing rests on visitor reports.

Those reports are consistent, and there are three of them. DOG·CLT's "Meet My
Dog" feature quotes a regular answering where to find a dog-friendly patio in
Charlotte: "Chester's a regular at Brawley's Beverage... there's also plenty of
water bowls, ample shade and limitless two- and four-legged friends for Chester
to meet every time we're there." Untappd's venue details flag the location Pet
Friendly. The Craft Beer Spots directory tags it Dog Friendly. None of the
three is the business speaking.

What that supports is dogs in the outdoor space, and that's all this listing
claims. Whether dogs come inside, where the coolers and the bar are, isn't
published or reported anywhere. Water bowls appear in one visitor account and
nowhere else, so the field above stays empty. Leash rules aren't published
either; county law requires physical restraint off your own property, and that
governs the patio regardless.

Hours are the other gap. Third-party listings show a fixed weekday schedule,
but the Instagram feed announces opening times day by day — "opening at 2pm"
one Friday, "opening at 3pm today" another. The same feed carries live music
nights and tap takeovers. The phone line is (704) 521-1300.

One Tripadvisor reviewer describes a section around the side of the building
with space for kids to play, which is the clearest published detail about how
the outdoor area is laid out. No Tripadvisor review mentions dogs at all.

Freedom Park is under two miles north up Park Road, with the Little Sugar Creek
Greenway running through it.
