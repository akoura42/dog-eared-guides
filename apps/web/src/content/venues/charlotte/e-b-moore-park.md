E.B. Moore Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park at 901 Marsh Road, in
Sedgefield. The county's parks ordinance is the document that governs dogs here,
and Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the
areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Dogs are allowed by
default. A posted sign is the only thing that narrows that, and the county
retired its per-park pages in the parkandrec.mecknc.gov rebuild, so nothing
specific to this park appears online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds. Water is the same story. Nothing published
mentions a fountain here one way or the other.

The built features sit at opposite ends, about 150 yards apart east to west. Two
tennis courts at the west end, a playground at the east end, open grass between
them. The county rents a picnic shelter on the property, listed in its ActiveNet
reservation system as "E.B. Moore Park - Small Shelter #1."

The county's park record leaves the size field blank, so there is no official
acreage; directories give it as four acres. Those directories also print the
full name as Edgar Blackburn Moore Park, while the county record reads only "EB
Moore Park." The closing time is posted at the park and published nowhere
online, and (980) 314-1002, the number in the county record, rings the Central
Region parks office rather than anything on site.

Marsh Road runs along the Dilworth edge of Sedgefield, between Park Road and
South Boulevard. Freedom Park is about a mile east off East Boulevard, and
Little Sugar Creek Greenway runs through it.
