Alexander Street Park sits off Interstate 277 northeast of uptown, six acres of
ball field, courts, and creek bottom. Mecklenburg County Park and Recreation
manages it. The county publishes no dog policy for this park, so the dog part of
this listing rests on visitor reports and trail directories rather than a posted
rule.

One official rule does cover ground inside the property. The Little Sugar Creek
Greenway runs through the middle of the park, and the county's published
greenway guidance is specific about it: "Dogs must be on a 6ft or shorter leash
and should be walked on the right edge of the trail." The same list asks that
waste go in a trash can. Beyond the trail, nothing. The tennis courts, the
soccer field, and the playground go unaddressed in anything Mecklenburg County
has put in writing about dogs here.

The segment through the park runs a mile one way, north through residential
blocks to Cordelia Park on North Davidson. Two bridges cross the wooded creek
inside the park itself. Carolina Thread Trail puts the trailhead at 745 East
12th Street and counts fifty parking spaces across the segment's three
trailheads, the other two at Parkwood Avenue and Cordelia Park.

The rest is a neighborhood park: six tennis courts with no lights, basketball,
a picnic shelter with a grill, restrooms, open field. Directories agree on that
list and disagree on smaller details, which is what happens when the source page
isn't readable.

Water for dogs isn't published one way or the other, and no listing mentions a
fountain. Restrooms are listed. At the north end, the greenway bridge crosses
toward Seigle Point, and Birdsong Brewing's taproom on North Davidson is about a
quarter mile up from there.
