Commonwealth Animal Hospital sits at 1909 Commonwealth Avenue, in the Plaza
Midwood residential grid where Commonwealth runs between Pecan and Thomas.
Dogs come in as patients. The one place the hospital's own site names them is
the boarding page: "We offer separate boarding facilities for cats and dogs so
our feline friends can have a little peace and quiet during their stay."

Hours are 7:30am to 6pm Monday through Friday, 7:30am to 1pm Saturday, closed
Sunday. Urgent care runs inside those hours, not outside them. The site's
definition: "Pets should go to urgent care for problems needing immediate
medical attention, even if they are not life-threatening." The page asks that
clients call the hospital first, at (704) 370-0767. There's also a text line,
(704) 459-7183, and reception@commonwealthah.com.

After hours the hospital publishes three referrals rather than taking the call
itself. CARE, Charlotte Animal Referral & Emergency, at 3726 Latrobe Drive,
(704) 457-2300, is the closest of the three, a couple of miles southeast. The
others are Carmel Road Animal Hospital, 7510 Pineville-Matthews Road #15a,
(704) 900-6039, and Livewell Animal Urgent Care of South End, 124 East
Kingston Avenue, Suite A, (704) 818-4839.

Boarding is in a "spacious, temperature-controlled" area with cats and dogs
kept apart, and drop-off and pick-up happen whenever the office is open.
Reservations are encouraged around holidays. Separately, the practice runs
drop-off appointments: leave the pet in the morning, collect by end of day.

Two domains carry this name. Commonwealthah.com is the live one, carrying the
services, hours, and booking. The address in the OpenStreetMap record,
commonwealthanimalhospital.net, is a legacy site still online over plain HTTP
with the same phone number and the same three veterinarians, no hours and no
boarding page.

Nothing published addresses leashes or carriers in the waiting room. County
restraint law applies regardless. Resident Culture's Plaza Midwood taproom is
about half a mile northeast, on Central Avenue.
