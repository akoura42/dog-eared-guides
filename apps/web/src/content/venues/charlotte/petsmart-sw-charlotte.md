PetSmart publishes its dog policy, so nothing here rests on visitor reports.
The chain's In-Store Pet Policy lists domestic dogs first among the pets
"permitted in PetSmart stores provided the pets are appropriately secured
(leashed or safely confined) and vaccinated (as appropriate for the particular
type of pet)." It applies to every location. This one is store #1509, the store
PetSmart labels SW Charlotte, at 14137 Rivergate Parkway.

The policy names no leash length. Leashed or safely confined is the whole of
it, which leaves Mecklenburg County's animal ordinance as the operative
restraint rule: physical restraint anywhere off your own property. Vaccination
is required in the same sentence, but no specific shot or paperwork is named.
Water bowls go unmentioned. And the policy ends on a reservation, that PetSmart
"reserves the right to prohibit any pet from being in a PetSmart location for
any reason."

A Banfield Pet Hospital operates inside this store, which changes the rule for
one group of dogs. Pets arriving for veterinary appointments "must be secured
in a crate, kennel, or other fastened enclosure to prevent exposure to the
public," and stay in it "until safely sheltered inside a hospital examination
room or kennel area." Walking in on a leash is what the policy describes for
shopping. Not for an appointment. Banfield runs 7 a.m. to 6 p.m. weekdays, 8 to
5 Saturday, 10 to 5 Sunday.

The store sits in the Rivergate center off South Tryon Street, which is NC 49
and a primary route open to heavy trucks. A McDonald's and a Chick-fil-A share
the immediate frontage, with Target, Best Buy and Home Depot around the rest of
the shared surface lot.

Walker Branch Greenway starts behind the shopping center, paved and close to
flat, and Mecklenburg County runs it.

Services published for #1509 are grooming and dog training. The salon opens at
7 a.m. Monday through Saturday and closes at 9, except Wednesday at 7 p.m., and
runs 8 to 6 on Sunday. Training's posted hours are 9 to 9 Monday through
Saturday, 10 to 7 Sunday. No PetsHotel and no Doggie Day Camp appear for this
store, which separates it from the Cotswold PetSmart, where boarding and day
camp run out of the same building. The South Boulevard store, which the chain
labels Pineville, is about six miles east.
