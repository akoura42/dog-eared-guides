Clanton Park is Mecklenburg County ground, so the county's rules are the park's
rules. The Parks & Recreation Facilities Ordinance, Section 26, makes it
unlawful "to have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly
prohibited." Nothing on the county's page for this park prohibits them. Dogs
are allowed on the grounds, leashed.

Park and Recreation's own trail rules put a number on the leash: "Pets must be
on leash 6 ft. or shorter," kept to the right of the path, and "Pick up after
your pet and deposit in trash can." The same rule sheet carries a line that
lands harder here than at most county parks. "Pets are not allowed in
playgrounds or athletic field areas." Read against what the county lists at
Clanton Park, that excludes a good share of the seventy acres. Basketball and
pickleball courts. A covered playground. A soccer field.

What's left is the greenway and the open ground between. The park's county page
describes "easy access to Irwin Creek greenway." The county's greenways index
files that same corridor under another name, listing 1.7 miles from West
Boulevard to Clanton Road as a section of Snow-Stewart Creek Greenway. One
path, two county sources, two names.

Irwin Creek itself runs through the property. Section 11 of the parks ordinance
bars bathing dogs in "the streams, lakes, or other water features of any park."

One building inside the boundary is closed to them outright. The Dorothy Doores
Waddy Pavilion at 3132 Manchester Drive, an indoor rental hall that seats up to
115, posts "No pets allowed except for trained service animals."

Off-leash isn't legal here. The county's designated dog parks are the only
off-leash ground in the Mecklenburg system, and the nearest is Frazier Park's,
about two and a half miles northeast at 1201 West Fourth Street Extension.

A county project will add a sprayground and may bring "repairs/expansion of
existing paved trails, wayfinding, and/or improvements to the parking area and
restroom." No construction dates are published, and the park appears on no
county closure list as of August 2026. The county publishes no posted closing
time for the park, and nothing about water fountains or bowls either way.
