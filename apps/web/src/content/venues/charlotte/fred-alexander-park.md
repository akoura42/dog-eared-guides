Fred Alexander Park is a Mecklenburg County park at 1832 Griers Grove Road, off
Beatties Ford Road in northwest Charlotte. The county's own facility page
confirms it runs the park, which puts the Park and Recreation Facilities
Ordinance in charge of the dog question. Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall
be unlawful to have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly
prohibited." Dogs are allowed by default. Posted signage is the only thing that
narrows that, and the county's page for this park lists every amenity without
mentioning dogs, pets or leashes anywhere.

No leash rule is published for the park. The parks ordinance has no leash
section at all, which leaves county Animal Ordinance Sec. 3-71 as the operative
rule: physical restraint anywhere off your own property. Water is the same
story. No fountain is mapped on the property and nothing published mentions one.

The park reopened in August 2024 after roughly $680,000 of work, part of a $7
million county equity allocation split across ten parks. Six acres. What that
money added: a pump track for bikes, outdoor workout equipment, accessible chess
and checkers tables, and a paved walking and biking loop, alongside the
playground, picnic shelter, community garden and basketball courts already
there. The county also lists a pickleball and tennis court, and signage about
Frederick Douglas Alexander, the civil rights leader the park is named for.

A block of mixed hardwood and evergreen covers one corner, the only wooded
ground on the property. Everything else is open. Ten benches sit along the
concrete walks, with a grill and two waste baskets near the shelter and an
uncovered four-space bike rack. The sidewalk crossings out to the street are
unmarked: no painted lines, no signal.

Closing time is posted at the park and published nowhere online. The number on
the county's page, (980) 314-1000, rings the Park and Recreation business office
at 5841 Brookshire Boulevard, weekdays 8 to 5, rather than anything on site.
Firestone Park sits a bit under a mile west.
