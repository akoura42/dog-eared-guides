Eagles Landing Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park at 1204 Eagles
Landing Drive, in the 28214 part of northwest Charlotte. The document that
governs dogs here is the county's Park and Recreation Facilities Ordinance.
Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas
of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Dogs are allowed by
default. Posted signage is the only thing that narrows that, and the county
publishes no page for this park at all: the two facility slugs matching its
name both 404, and so does the older county URL still recorded against the park
boundary in OpenStreetMap.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. The parks ordinance has no leash
section, which leaves county animal ordinance Sec. 3-71 as the operative one:
physical restraint anywhere off your own property. A drinking fountain is
mapped inside the park, roughly fifty feet east of the playground. Whether it
runs isn't published, and nothing published mentions bowls.

The park sits inside the subdivision it's named for. Eagles Landing Drive
fronts it, with Eagle Rock Lane, Eagle Ridge Drive, and Silver Eagle Drive
running off nearby. Residential streets on every side, no arterial. No parking
lot is mapped on the property, which leaves the street.

What's there is compact: a playground, two benches, two trash cans, and paved
walks that meet the neighborhood sidewalk at unmarked crossings. Mown grass
fills the rest. The county's record classes the park "Developed" and leaves the
size field blank, so there is no official acreage.

A stream runs past the property and drops into culverts on both sides of it,
and an infiltration basin nearby holds water only intermittently. Section 11 of
the parks ordinance covers that water: no bathing dogs "in the streams, lakes,
or other water features of any park." The closing time under Section 31 is
posted at the park and published nowhere online, and (980) 314-1001, the number
in the county record, rings the North Region parks office rather than anything
on site.

Shuffletown Dog Park, the nearest designated off-leash ground, is about three
and a half miles north on Bellhaven Boulevard.
