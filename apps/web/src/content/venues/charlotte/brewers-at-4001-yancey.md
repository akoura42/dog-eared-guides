Brewers at 4001 Yancey sits on Yancey Road in Lower South End, in the low
warehouse belt southwest of the light rail. The brewery answers the dog
question on its own FAQ page, and the answer starts with a no before it gets
to the yes: "While dogs are not allowed indoors, we have a beer garden, a
covered patio, and a partially enclosed patio for our fur friends. Even
better, we have an enclosed dog run for our beer buddies to play!"

Indoors is out. That part is written down rather than left to inference, which
is unusual enough to be worth noting flatly.

What's left is a lot. Three outdoor rooms, not one row of sidewalk tables. The
beer garden is the one you'll spot from the lot: blue shade sails strung
overhead, twinkle lighting, and a bocce court fenced in wrought iron. One patio
is covered, which handles rain and the four o'clock sun. A third is partially
enclosed. The dog run is fenced and separate from the seating.

Leashes and water bowls aren't published — not on the FAQ, not anywhere else
on the site. County leash law is the operative rule on the patios.

Thirty-five to forty taps run at once, drawn from Victory, Sixpoint, Southern
Tier, and Bold Rock, alongside beers brewed on site under the CLT Brew'd name.
The current tap list lives on Untappd rather than on the brewery's own pages,
so what's actually pouring today isn't something the site will tell you.

The kitchen is a real one, running wings and pizzas and steak frites, and the
FAQ says outside food isn't permitted. All ages are welcome in the building.
Hours are 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Monday through Saturday, 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Sunday.

Olde Mecklenburg's biergarten is a few minutes up the same road.
