Petco's dog policy is corporate, not store-level. No Petco store page publishes
one, and #1526 is no exception. The company's newsroom is where the policy
actually lives: pet parents "can always bring leashed pets of all breeds,
shapes and sizes to Petco stores, also known as 'cooling centers,' to seek
refuge from extreme heat," and "Petco locations nationwide provide pets with
clean, fresh water during regular business hours." That language is dated May
19, 2026. A 2023 release fills in the mechanics, describing cool drinking water
served in disposable bowls and shade inside the stores.

Dogs on the sales floor, then, leashed. No leash length appears in either
release, which leaves Mecklenburg County's animal ordinance as the operative
rule: physical restraint anywhere off your own property.

The store sits at the Providence Road and Pineville-Matthews Road corner, in
the Arboretum. Its address is the NC-51 side, 3401, while the shopping center
lists itself at 8008 Providence. Parking is the shared center lot.

This is the Charlotte Petco with a veterinary hospital in it. The other one,
#2533 on Metropolitan Avenue in Midtown, has no hospital. The store page here
lists full-service grooming, dog training, a vaccination clinic, the hospital,
and an aquatics department, plus adoptions through Petco Love. What isn't
published anywhere readable is whether the hospital handles arriving patients
differently from shoppers walking the aisles, or what vaccinations the grooming
salon requires.

Hours as posted: 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday, 9 to 8 on Sunday.

Nothing else on this site is close. The Cotswold PetSmart is about six miles
north, and the nearest published dog-friendly patio is farther than that.
