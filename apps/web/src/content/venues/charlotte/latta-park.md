Latta Park is a Mecklenburg County park, so the county's parks ordinance is
what governs dogs here. Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to
have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited."
Dogs are allowed by default. A posted sign is the only thing that narrows that,
and the county retired its per-park pages in the parkandrec.mecknc.gov
redesign, so nothing specific to Latta appears online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds. Park and Recreation's six-foot leash rule is
written for greenways, and the county's own park record marks Latta with no
greenway access, so it doesn't reach these paths. Water bowls, the county says
nothing about in either direction.

Off-leash isn't legal here. The county park record sets the dog-park field to
No, and the county's designated dog parks are the only off-leash ground in
Mecklenburg County. Frazier Park's is the closest, about a mile and three
quarters north.

The sprayground is the part that changes with the calendar. The county lists it
open daily from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m., Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day. It's
a water feature, and Section 11 of the parks ordinance covers those directly:
no bathing dogs "in the streams, lakes, or other water features of any park."

From the county's record: thirty-two parking spaces, restrooms, a picnic
shelter, playground with swings, basketball, tennis and volleyball courts,
walking paths, a fitness area, and open green space. Hours are listed as
daylight, seven days. Acreage is left blank in the county record; directories
give it as 31 acres.

The address point, 601 East Park Avenue, sits at the northwest end. The park
runs east from there, a bit over half a mile end to end. The county rec center
in walking distance, Tom Sykes on Dilworth Elementary's Latta campus, is closed
until September 2027 for school construction.

300 East is about a third of a mile west on East Boulevard. Freedom Park is a
little over a mile south, and the Little Sugar Creek Greenway runs about a mile
east.
