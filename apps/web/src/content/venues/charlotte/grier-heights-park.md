Grier Heights Park is a six-acre Mecklenburg County park on Leroy Street,
adjacent to Billingsville Cotswold Elementary School. Dogs are allowed. The
county parks ordinance handles pets in a single sentence, Sec. 26: "It shall be
unlawful to have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly
prohibited." Allowed by default, narrowed only where the county says otherwise.

One place here does say otherwise. The park holds one of the roughly twenty
community gardens the county runs, and the rules published for all of them read:
"No pets (only service animals are allowed. Please clean up after your service
animals; bag and remove off site.)" That language covers the garden plots. It
doesn't reach the rest of the grounds.

No leash rule is published for the park itself. County animal ordinance Sec.
3-71 requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it
the operative rule out here. Water is the same story. Nothing published mentions
a drinking fountain, and the county says nothing about bowls either way.

What the county lists on the property: baseball fields, a half basketball court,
an amphitheater it renovated in late 2023, the community gardens, a playground,
and a picnic shelter. Grier Heights is also one of six county neighborhood parks
carrying free public Wi-Fi.

The Briar Creek Greenway does not reach the park. A county reinvestment project
lists "an accessible bike/pedestrian connection to Briar Creek Greenway" among
possible improvements, along with expanded gardens and fitness stations, with
design work starting in the summer of 2026. Nothing there is built yet.

The county retired its per-park pages in the parkandrec.mecknc.gov redesign, so
no page for this park exists and no site-specific pet prohibition beyond the
garden appears anywhere online. Closing time is posted at the park and published
nowhere online. No park phone number is published either.

The old Billingsville School building stands a block north on the same street
and now operates as the Grier Heights Community Center.

Eastover Park sits about a third of a mile west.
