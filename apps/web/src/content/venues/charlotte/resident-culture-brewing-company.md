Resident Culture Brewing Company at 2101 Central Avenue is the brewery proper:
a revitalized warehouse where all the company's beer is made. Its FAQ answers
the pet question in one line. "Yes! We allow well behaved, leashed pets both
inside and outside our Brewery taproom." Inside counts, which is rarer than
the patio-only rule most Charlotte taprooms publish.

The outdoor side comes in parts, and the brewery names them on its private
events page: a Barrel Corner, an open-air Beer Garden Patio, a Beer Garden
Tent held back for private bookings, and outdoor patio tables. The tent is the
piece that can be closed to the public on a given night. Nothing on the site
says the pet rule changes from one space to the next.

Food is a truck. Leroy's Snack Shack parks on site and runs 4:30 to 9 Tuesday
through Thursday, 4:30 to 9:30 Friday and Saturday, noon to 7 Sunday. The
taproom's own hours run longer than that, so the beer outlasts the kitchen
most nights. Sandwiches, wings, loaded fries, per the FAQ. No dish list is
published anywhere on the site.

Water bowls are not published one way or the other. The bowls-and-free-treats
detail that circulates in directory listings doesn't appear on any Resident
Culture page. Leashes are the brewery's written condition, and county law
requires physical restraint off your own property regardless.

One scope note. That FAQ answer says "Brewery taproom," and the brewery is
this address. Resident Culture's other location, a taproom inside the Market
at 7th Street food hall uptown, isn't named in the answer, and its own page
says nothing about pets.

Hours on Central Avenue: 3pm to 10pm Monday through Thursday, 3 to 11 Friday,
noon to 11 Saturday, noon to 8 Sunday.
