Petco's dog policy is corporate, not store-level. No Petco store page publishes
one, and #2533 is no exception. The company newsroom is where the language
actually sits: pet parents "can always bring leashed pets of all breeds, shapes
and sizes to Petco stores, also known as 'cooling centers,' to seek refuge from
extreme heat," and "Petco locations nationwide provide pets with clean, fresh
water during regular business hours." That release is dated May 19, 2026. A 2023
release fills in the mechanics, describing cool drinking water served in
disposable bowls, which Petco says "helps limit the risk of disease
transmission," and shade inside the stores.

Leashed dogs on the sales floor, then. No leash length appears in either
release, which leaves Mecklenburg County's animal ordinance as the operative
rule: physical restraint anywhere off your own property.

The store shares 900 Metropolitan Avenue with Target, which occupies Suite 2 at
the same address. It's on the Midtown side of I-277, and Little Sugar Creek
Greenway runs along the edge of the property. The Metropolitan center next door
carries a different address, 1111 Metropolitan, and reserves levels P1 through
P3 of its parking deck for Metropolitan visitors; Petco isn't in that center's
tenant directory.

Services as posted on the store page: full-service dog grooming, dog training
classes, a vaccination clinic, and an aquatics department. Adoptions run through
Petco Love with local welfare partners at select times. No veterinary hospital
at this one. What the grooming salon requires for proof of vaccination is
published only on www.petco.com, which is CAPTCHA-walled, so it stays unlisted
here.

Hours as posted: 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday, 9 to 8 on Sunday.

The other Charlotte Petco, #1526, is about nine miles south in the Arboretum,
and that's the one with the vet hospital in it.
