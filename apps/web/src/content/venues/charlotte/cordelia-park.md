Cordelia Park sits at the north end of the Little Sugar Creek Greenway, in
Villa Heights. Mecklenburg County owns it and carries it as a developed
community park in the Central district. Dogs are allowed. The county's Park and
Recreation Facilities Ordinance handles pets in a single line, Sec. 26: "It
shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are
expressly prohibited." Allowed by default, narrowed only by posted signs.

One exclusion is published. The Cordelia Park Indoor Pavilion, the reservable
building overlooking the pool, states "No pets allowed except for trained
service animals." A park ranger opens and secures that building around events.

The greenway carries the county's only published leash language: "Dogs must be
on a 6ft or shorter leash and should be walked on the right edge of the trail."
The same list asks that waste go in a trash can. Nothing published addresses
the playground, the pool deck, or the rest of the grounds, which leaves
Mecklenburg County's animal ordinance and its requirement of physical restraint
anywhere off your own property.

Two addresses. The park is 600 East 24th Street; the pool has its own entrance
at 2100 North Davidson. The upper parking lot sits next to the playground,
uphill from the pavilion, which is where the county's cleanup crews meet.
Cordelia Pool runs through Labor Day, September 7 this year, noon to 6 in two
sessions, weekends only from August 24. Admission is $2 for county residents,
$3 otherwise. The sprayground runs 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. daily from Memorial Day
weekend to Labor Day. Water for dogs isn't published one way or the other, and
no county page lists a fountain.

The greenway itself runs over seventeen miles, Brevard Street to the South
Carolina state line, part of the Cross Charlotte Trail. Southbound it covers a
mile of residential blocks before reaching Alexander Street Park. Free Range
Brewing is two blocks up North Davidson from the pool entrance. The posted
closing time isn't published online anywhere, and Sec. 31 makes staying past it
unlawful.
