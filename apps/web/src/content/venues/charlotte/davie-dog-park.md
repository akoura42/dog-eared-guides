Davie Dog Park is the county's off-leash area inside William R. Davie Park, a
107-acre regional park at 4635 Pineville-Matthews Road in south Charlotte. It
gets its own page under Park and Recreation's dog parks section. That page
carries the name and a map link, nothing more. The rules live on the section
index and are written for the whole program rather than for this site.

They run short. County dog parks are "open daily from 7:30 a.m. to sunset," so
closing slides across the year. Vaccinations are a condition of entry, in the
county's wording "Rabies with Tag Number, DHLPP & Bordetella," required "to
utilize the off-leash areas." No children under the age of 12 are allowed.
Owners "must follow all posted dog park rules," which makes the sign at the
gate the document that counts once you're through it.

Off-leash stops at the fence. Across the remaining acreage, county animal
ordinance Sec. 3-71 and its physical-restraint requirement is the rule, past
the ball fields, the playground, the volleyball court and the walking paths.

The park entrance is off NC 51. The drive runs back past a lot near the
recycling center to a second lot at the south end, and the fenced areas sit a
few hundred feet further south of that one, down a slope in tree cover.

What the county doesn't publish about this dog park: acreage, how many fenced
sections there are, whether they're divided by dog size, water, shade, a fee.
Directories describe two runs split at twenty pounds. None of that appears in
county material, so this listing doesn't carry it. What the county does
publish is that design work has started "for improvements to the existing dog
park that will address accessibility and maintainability." No timeline, and no
notice about closing during the work.

An unofficial Facebook place page called "McMullen Dog Park" drops a pin on
the south lot here. The county operates no facility under that name.

Barkingham Park at Reedy Creek and Ninth Street Dog Park in Fourth Ward run
under the identical county rules.
