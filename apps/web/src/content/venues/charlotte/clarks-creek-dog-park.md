Clarks Creek Dog Park is the off-leash area inside Clarks Creek Community Park,
a Mecklenburg County community park at 5435 Hucks Road in north Charlotte,
roughly nine miles north of Uptown. The county GIS parks record lists it as
county-owned and developed, in the North region. The rules come from the
county's dog parks page, and they're written for the whole system rather than
for this site.

They run short. County dog parks are "open daily from 7:30 a.m. to sunset," so
closing slides through the year. Vaccination is a condition of entry, in the
county's words "dogs must meet all guidelines and maintain current vaccinations
(Rabies with Tag Number, DHLPP & Bordetella) to utilize the off-leash areas."
No children under the age of 12 are allowed. Owners "must follow all posted dog
park rules," which puts the sign at the gate above anything published online.

The county's per-park page for Clarks Creek Dog Park loads as a heading over a
map placeholder. Nothing under it. So the specifics stay blank here.
OpenStreetMap maps two adjoining enclosures, tagged Large Dogs Park and Small
Dogs Park, the small one sitting just north of the large one. The county
publishes neither that split, nor the fenced acreage, nor anything about water
or a fee.

Outside the enclosures the ordinance governs. Section 26 of the Park and
Recreation Facilities Ordinance reads, "It shall be unlawful to have pets in
the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited," which leaves
dogs allowed by default on the grounds. No leash rule is published for the park
itself, so the county animal ordinance applies there: physical restraint
anywhere off your own property. Section 11 bars bathing dogs in park water
features. Section 31 makes staying past the posted closing time unlawful, and
that time appears nowhere online.

Clarks Creek Nature Preserve sits directly across Hucks Road at 5434, a
separate county property carried under its own designation in the same GIS
layer. Nothing about dogs is published for it either way.

The county's park record leaves the size field blank, so there is no official
acreage; directories give the park as 32.5 acres. (980) 314-1001 rings the
North Region parks office rather than a phone on site.
