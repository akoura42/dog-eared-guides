Grayson Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park at 750 Beal Street, in
Grier Heights. The parks ordinance is the document that governs dogs here, and
Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas
of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Dogs are allowed by
default. Posted signage is the only thing that narrows that, and the county
publishes nothing specific to Grayson: the `/facility/grayson-park` page 404s,
and the Naomi Drenan Recreation Center page, which covers the building on this
property, says nothing about pets.

Leash rules for the park aren't published either. County animal ordinance Sec.
3-71 requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it
the operative rule on the grounds.

The park wraps around that recreation center, and the ground inside it is
mostly programmed. A lit baseball diamond and a batting cage at the east end. A
four-hoop paved basketball court in the middle, two tennis courts at the south
edge, a picnic shelter, and playground equipment in two separate sections. Two
parking lots open off Beal Street, one concrete and one asphalt.

Grayson Skatepark takes up the west end, behind fencing, closest to the rec
center. The county's release on the reopening puts the whole project at $4.78
million and lists what changed: bowl repairs, a new elevated mini bowl, a
volcano feature, new fencing, shade structures and bleachers, plus a skate
plaza connecting to the building. That surface charges a daily fee, $5 adult
and $2 youth for county residents, and requires pads, a helmet, and a signed
waiver.

A drinking fountain is mapped inside the park near the tennis courts. Nothing
published says whether it runs, and nothing published mentions a dog bowl.

The county's park record leaves the size field blank, so there's no official
acreage; directories put it at 12.1 acres. Closing time is posted at the park
and published nowhere online. The number in the county record, (980) 314-1003,
rings the South Region parks office rather than anything on site; the rec
center desk is (980) 314-1100, staffed 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through
Thursday, 8 to 6 Friday, 9 to 5 Saturday, 1 to 5 Sunday.

Eastover Park sits a little over a mile west. PetSmart Cotswold is about a mile
south.
