First Ward Park is a Mecklenburg County park at 309 East 7th Street, on the
corner of 7th and Brevard in Uptown. Section 26 of the county's parks ordinance
governs dogs here, and it runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have pets
in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Dogs are
allowed by default. Posted signage is the only thing that narrows that, and the
county publishes no per-park page for First Ward, so nothing specific to this
park appears online either way.

The county has said one thing about dogs here directly. A Park and Recreation
blog post from June 21, 2019, rounding up dog-friendly summer locations, put the
park's Sounds of Summer concerts on the list: "your leashed dog is welcome to
join you!"

No leash requirement is published for the park itself. County animal ordinance
Sec. 3-71 requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which
makes it the operative rule on the grounds. Water bowls aren't mentioned
anywhere.

Two fountains and a sprayground sit on the property. Section 11 of the parks
ordinance covers all of it: no bathing dogs "in the streams, lakes, or other
water features of any park."

The park opened December 8, 2015, on 4.6 acres by the designer's account. The
county's own park record leaves the size field blank, so there's no official
acreage. The middle is an open lawn, sloping gently enough to work as an
amphitheater, with no tree canopy over it. Shade sits at the edges, under the
arbor and the perimeter plantings. A garden walking circuit rings the park, with
seat walls of native Carolina granite set along it, and an outdoor fitness zone
with equipment free to use.

The LYNX Blue Line runs at grade along Brevard Street on the park's edge,
between the 7th Street and 9th Street stations. Trains pass all day. ImaginOn is
across 7th Street, and UNC Charlotte's Center City campus is a block up Brevard.
Parking is metered, on Brevard and 8th.

Closing time is posted at the park and published nowhere online. The (980)
314-1002 number in the county record rings the Central Region parks office
rather than anything on site.

Ninth Street Dog Park, the nearest off-leash ground, is about half a mile
northwest in Fourth Ward. Alexander Street Park is roughly the same distance
east.
