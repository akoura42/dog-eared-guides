Dowd Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park on the block bound by
Monument Street, Greenland Avenue and Remount Road, in Camp Greene. Dogs are
allowed. The county's Park and Recreation Facilities Ordinance handles pets in
a single line, Sec. 26: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of
County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Allowed by default, narrowed
only where a sign says otherwise. The county retired its per-park pages in the
parkandrec.mecknc.gov redesign, so nothing specific to Dowd appears online
either way.

No leash rule is published for the park. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds. Dowd isn't one of the eight off-leash dog parks
the county lists, so there's no fenced area here where that lifts.

The white house on the corner is not part of the park. That's the James C. Dowd
House, 2216 Monument Street, restored and occupied since March 2023 as the
corporate office of MECA Commercial Real Estate, which lists the address as its
own. Private lawn, private lot. The park is the open ground beside it.

All of it is new. The county built the park out from a vacant lot and finished
it in summer 2022, and its own project page describes "a playground, a shelter,
walking paths, benches and trees." Two playground areas are mapped, along with
a shelter building, concrete walks, benches across the property, a grill, bike
racks and a drinking fountain. Whether that fountain runs, and whether anything
at it sits at dog height, isn't published either way. Nothing published
describes the tree canopy, and the plantings date to the 2022 build.

No parking lot is mapped. Monument, Greenland and Remount are residential
streets on all three sides, and parking is on-street.

The number in the county record, (980) 314-1002, rings the Central Region parks
office rather than anything on site. Closing time is posted at the park and
published nowhere online.

Abbott Park is about a mile southeast, in Wilmore. Clanton Park sits a little
over a mile due south, off Clanton Road.
