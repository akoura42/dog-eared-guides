Eva B. Barber Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park in
Thomasboro-Hoskins, and the county's parks ordinance is the document that
governs dogs here. Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have
pets in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Dogs
are allowed by default. Posted signage is the only thing that narrows that, and
the county retired its per-park pages in the parkandrec.mecknc.gov redesign, so
nothing specific to Barber appears online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds. Drinking water is the same story. No fountain is
mapped on the property, and nothing published mentions one.

The county announced a renovation here in a release dated July 30, 2025. The
park "now features a new inclusive playground, an accessible shelter, and
pathways connecting the park amenities," plus new trees planted in partnership
with TreesCharlotte. Those pathways are concrete and short. They link the built
pieces to each other; no loop or trail is mapped anywhere on the property.

What sits on the ground: a basketball court with two hoops, a sand volleyball
court, the playground, a picnic shelter, and a gazebo.

Four residential streets box the park in — Gossett Avenue, South Linwood
Avenue, Cromer Street, and South Crigler Street. That frontage is why the
address comes back differently depending on where you look. The county's own
parks record gives 3801 Gossett Ave, 28208. Directories print 301 Cromer
Street. No parking lot is mapped, and every street around it is residential.

Nothing on the property holds water. Stewart Creek runs a few hundred yards
off, outside the park.

The county's park record leaves the size field blank, so there is no official
acreage. The closing time is posted at the park and published nowhere online,
and (980) 314-1001, the number in the county record, rings the North Region
parks office rather than anything on site.

Dowd Park is about two and a half miles south, straight down this side of town.
