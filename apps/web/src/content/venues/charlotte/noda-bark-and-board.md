NoDa Bark & Board is a day care and boarding facility, so the dog question isn't
whether dogs are allowed. It's whether a given dog clears intake. The onboarding
page lists four required vaccinations: rabies, DHLPP/DLPP/DHPP, canine
influenza, and bordetella, which the site spells "Bordatella." Every dog must be
spayed or neutered. The business ties that to a North Carolina Department of
Agriculture rule it dates to September 2022. And puppies under six months are
not accepted, a change the page marks as recent: "We can no longer accept
puppies under 6 months."

No breed list. No weight cap. Nothing published about a temperament evaluation
or a trial day before the first full booking, and nothing about how vaccination
records get to the front desk.

The schedule has a hole in the middle of it. Weekdays run 7 a.m. to 7 p.m., but
the building closes daily from 11 to 3 for nap time. Saturdays and Sundays are
boarding drop-off and pick-up only, no day care: 9 a.m. to noon Saturday, 4 to 6
p.m. Sunday.

Day care is $38 for the first dog, $30 for each additional, with passes down to
$32 a day at ten and an unlimited monthly at $475. Overnight boarding is $55,
$40 for a second dog. CMS teachers and first responders get a discount with ID.
Play runs in groups sorted by age, size, and temperament, indoor and outdoor,
morning and afternoon, with a two-hour rest in between. Staff administer
medications. Boarding dogs get a cot rather than the floor, bowls, and an
evening relief walk.

The address puts it on the 2000 block of North Davidson, at the Optimist Park
end of the street rather than the Davidson-and-36th blocks a mile north.
Birdsong Brewing is about a mile the other direction on the same street.

The contact form carries its own warning: "Please allow up to 48 hours for
response." Email is nodabark@gmail.com.
