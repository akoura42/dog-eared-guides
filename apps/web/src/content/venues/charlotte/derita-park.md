Mecklenburg County's own records call this park Derita Park. OSM, Yelp, and the
directories call it Derita Creek Park. Same 23.6 acres, same address at 3509
Frew Road.

Dogs are allowed here by default. The county's parks ordinance is the governing
document, and Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have pets
in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Posted
signage is the only thing that narrows that. The county has no per-park page
for this property, so nothing specific to Derita appears online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds. Water is the same story. No fountain is mapped
on the property, and nothing published mentions one.

The built parts sit together in one corner. A paved basketball court with two
hoops, a playground, a picnic shelter, and a small parking lot, all at the
south end near West Craighead Road, linked by a short unpaved path. The rest of
the acreage is undeveloped. Several streams and drainage channels cross it, one
of them dropping into a culvert. Section 11 of the parks ordinance covers that
water directly: no bathing dogs "in the streams, lakes, or other water features
of any park."

The county's capital plan has work queued here. "This project will improve and
expand recreation amenities at Derita Park, an existing 23.6-acre park in
northeast Charlotte," with the new amenities set through community engagement
rather than named in advance. The existing amenities it lists are the
basketball court, playground, picnic area, and shelter.

Closing time is posted at the park and published nowhere online. The number in
the county record, (980) 314-1001, rings the North Region parks office rather
than anything on site.

Druid Hills Park is about a mile and a half southwest. NoDa Brewing's North End
taproom sits roughly a mile south.
