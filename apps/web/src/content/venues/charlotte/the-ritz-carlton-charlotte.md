The Ritz-Carlton, Charlotte takes pets, and says so itself. Three lines on the
property page, no more: "Pets Welcome," a "Non-Refundable Pet Fee Per Stay" of
$250.00, and a "Maximum Number of Pets in Room" of 1.

What isn't published matters too. No weight cap, no breed list, and no
statement about which parts of the building a dog may cross. Nothing about a
relief area. Nothing about water bowls either way. No leash rule appears
anywhere on the hotel's pages, which leaves Mecklenburg County's ordinance as
the operative one on the sidewalk: physical restraint anywhere off your own
property.

The hotel holds the corner of Trade and College, in Uptown. The CityLYNX Gold
Line streetcar runs along Trade directly out front, and the arena is a block
east. Parking, as published, is valet only, $50 a day. Check-in is 4 p.m.,
checkout noon.

Dining is mid-renovation. The main restaurant is closed for work, so The Fifth
Fork operates on the fifteenth floor, 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. most days. Sweet July
Cafe runs 6:30 a.m. to 8 p.m. weekdays and 7 to 8 on weekends. Bourbon Steak,
Michael Mina's steakhouse, is listed for winter 2026. None of the three
advertises outdoor seating, and the hotel describes no patio anywhere on the
property.

Some of the building's own details: it was the first LEED Gold-certified hotel
in greater Charlotte and the first for The Ritz-Carlton Hotel Company. There
are apiaries on the roof and a garden the kitchen cooks from. The pool is
bromine-treated instead of chlorinated. The spa occupies two penthouse floors.

Green space is a walk. Romare Bearden Park sits about half a mile west, at
Church and Fourth. The nearest access to the Little Sugar Creek Greenway is
roughly a mile east, past I-277.
