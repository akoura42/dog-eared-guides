Dogs are allowed inside, and PetSmart says so in writing. The chain's In-Store
Pet Policy lists domestic dogs among the pets "permitted in PetSmart stores
provided the pets are appropriately secured (leashed or safely confined) and
vaccinated (as appropriate for the particular type of pet)." It applies to
every location. This one is store #408, at 5401 South Boulevard, Suite 14.

Leashed or safely confined is the whole of the restraint rule. No length, no
gauge, nothing about retractables. Mecklenburg County's animal ordinance is
what governs past that point: physical restraint anywhere off your own
property. Water bowls appear nowhere in anything PetSmart publishes.

Two further lines sit at the end of the policy. Pets outside its permitted list
"are not permitted inside any PetSmart store location unless the pet is a
service animal under the federal guidelines of the Americans with Disabilities
Act" or has an appointment with an in-store veterinary hospital. And PetSmart
"reserves the right to prohibit any pet from being in a PetSmart location for
any reason."

The store sits in Tyvola Square, a 186,085-square-foot center at South
Boulevard and Tyvola Road that its landlord describes as being on "a major
commercial corridor in Lower South End." Ross Dress for Less, Ashley
HomeStore, Five Below and K&G Fashion Superstore share the strip. Parking is
the center's shared surface lot, hard against the I-77 interchange, and the
LYNX Blue Line's Tyvola Station is about half a mile off.

PetSmart's locator lists grooming and dog training here. No veterinary hospital
appears among them, which separates #408 from the Pineville store about four
miles south, where a Banfield operates inside the building and vet patients
come in crated rather than leashed.

Hours as posted: 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through Saturday, 10 to 7 Sunday. The
grooming salon runs on its own clock, opening at 7 a.m. Monday through Saturday
and 8 on Sunday, closing at 8 most nights, 7 on Wednesday and 6 on Sunday.

Archdale Animal Hospital is under a mile south, same side of South Boulevard.
