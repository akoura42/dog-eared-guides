Marshall Park is a Mecklenburg County park at 800 East Third Street, in Second
Ward. Dogs are allowed. The county's Park and Recreation Facilities Ordinance
handles pets in a single sentence, Sec. 26: "It shall be unlawful to have pets
in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Allowed by
default, narrowed only where a sign says otherwise. The county retired its
per-park pages in the parkandrec.mecknc.gov redesign, and the address for this
one returns a 404, so nothing specific to Marshall appears online either way.

No leash rule is published for the park itself. Charlotte Animal Care & Control
puts the county requirement plainly: dog owners walking dogs "in their
neighborhoods and/or in public parks (not designated as a dog park) are
required to keep their dogs on leash and under physical restraint at ALL
TIMES." Marshall Park is not one of the county's designated dog parks, which
are separate facilities carrying their own posted rules and vaccination
requirements. Nothing published mentions water bowls or fountains here.

The pond is the middle of the park, a little over an acre of it, with a
fountain and a footbridge crossing the water. Paved walkways run the loop
around it. Section 11 of the parks ordinance speaks to that water directly: no
bathing dogs "in the streams, lakes, or other water features of any park."
Wading and swimming are barred outside designated swim areas.

Around the pond sit open lawn, an amphitheater, a statue of Martin Luther King
Jr., and a Holocaust memorial, with the skyline standing up across the water.
The county's record puts the property at 5.5 acres.

Closing time is posted at the park and published nowhere online. Sec. 31 makes
leaving before it a legal requirement rather than a courtesy. No phone number
specific to the park is published; the Park and Recreation business office
line is 980-314-1000.

The land is committed to the Brooklyn Village redevelopment. The county's own
announcement places the park in a later phase: "Later phases will be located at
the site of the former Board of Education building and Marshall Park." Phase I
covers the former Walton Plaza building and its parking lot instead. No closure
date for the park has been published.

Little Sugar Creek Greenway runs a few blocks east. First Ward Park is about
half a mile north.
