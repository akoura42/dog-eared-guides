Lakewood Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park on Kalynne Street, in
the Lakewood neighborhood west of uptown. Dogs here are governed by the
county's Park and Recreation Facilities Ordinance, and Section 26 runs one
sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of County parks where
pets are expressly prohibited." Allowed by default, in other words. Posted
signage is the only thing that narrows that, and the county publishes no page
for this park, so nothing specific to Lakewood is documented online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds. Water is the same story. No fountain is mapped
on the property, and nothing published mentions one.

The developed part sits in a tight cluster: a softball field at the north end,
a basketball court in the middle, a playground on the south side, concrete
walks running between them. Kalynne Street runs along one edge, Ramona and
Jones streets around the rest, residential blocks in every direction. No
parking lot is mapped on the property. A chain of small ponds sits just west of
the park, outside the boundary as OpenStreetMap draws it.

The county's own park record leaves the size field blank, so there is no
official acreage; directories give it as 11 acres. The address is unsettled
too. The county record reads 3207 Kalynne Street, while Yelp and recplanet both
print 3131. And (980) 314-1002, the number in the county record, rings the
Central Region parks office rather than anything on site.

Closing time is posted at the park and published nowhere online. Section 31 of
the parks ordinance covers it directly: it is "unlawful for any person to fail
to leave a park prior to the posted closing time for that park."

Eva B. Barber Park is about half a mile northwest, in Thomasboro-Hoskins. Five
Points Park sits roughly a mile east, in Biddleville.
