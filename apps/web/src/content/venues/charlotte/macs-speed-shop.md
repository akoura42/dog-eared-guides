The dog language at Mac's sits on the company story page rather than the South
End location page. Mac's Speed Shop, it says, has "live music, events and a
dog-friendly patio so you can bring the whole family." That's brand copy,
written to cover all eight Mac's. South End is the first of them, open since
May 2005 in a former motor shop on South Boulevard.

Patio only, as published. Nothing on macspeedshop.com puts dogs indoors, and
the state rule that makes patio dogs legal in North Carolina, 15A NCAC 18A
.2656, allows them in outdoor dining areas "provided that dogs and cats are
physically restrained, and do not pass through any indoor areas of the food
establishment." Mac's publishes no leash rule of its own. Not published means
the county rule is the one that governs: Mecklenburg's animal ordinance
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, enforced by CMPD
Animal Care & Control.

Water bowls, size or breed limits, a pet fee, whether any of the patio is
covered. None of it appears on the site. The location page publishes hours and
a phone number and stops there. Sunday through Thursday, 11 a.m. to 11 p.m.
Friday and Saturday until midnight. There's an events calendar for this
location, though the site doesn't say whether the music plays on the patio or
inside.

The building still reads as the garage it was, which is the point. Behind the
bar are over forty beers on draft and a hundred bottles and cans, updated
daily. The barbecue is hickory-smoked: pulled pork, Angus brisket, St. Louis
ribs, chicken on the bone. Motorcycles are the house theme, and Mac's own line
for who shows up is "whether you're riding a Harley or driving a minivan."

South Boulevard carries the LYNX Blue Line alongside it here, so the ambient
sound at an outdoor table is arterial traffic and light rail, not a side
street. Freedom Park is about a mile and a half east, where the same county
restraint rule shows up as Park and Recreation's posted six-foot leash.
