Eastway Park is a Mecklenburg County community park at 423 Eastway Drive, and
the document that governs dogs here is the county's Park and Recreation
Facilities Ordinance. Section 26 runs one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to
have pets in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited."
Dogs are allowed by default. Posted signage is the only thing that narrows
that, and the county retired its per-park pages in the parkandrec.mecknc.gov
redesign, so nothing specific to Eastway appears online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. The parks ordinance has no leash
section, which leaves county animal ordinance Sec. 3-71 as the operative one:
physical restraint anywhere off your own property. Water is the same story.
No drinking fountain is mapped on the property, and nothing published mentions
one.

Eastway Regional Recreation Center sits in the middle of the park, at 3150
Eastway Park Drive, open since April 2021. Indoor pool, fitness floor, gym. The
county's page for it publishes nothing about dogs one way or the other. Outside
the front doors, a Little Free Library and two benches sit maybe twenty feet
from the entrance.

The rest of the property is outdoor. An artificial-turf soccer pitch sits
southwest of the rec center, open field space runs east of that, wooded patches
line the edges, a restroom building stands near the middle, and surface lots
front Eastway Park Drive. A basin on the northeast side holds water
intermittently. Lake Plaza, a reservoir, sits just off the south boundary, and
Section 11 of the parks ordinance covers both: no bathing dogs "in the streams,
lakes, or other water features of any park."

The county's park record leaves the size field blank, so there is no official
acreage. News coverage and directories give it as 95 acres. The closing time is
posted at the park and published nowhere online. And (980) 314-1001, the number
in the county record, rings a regional parks office rather than anything on
site; the rec center desk is (980) 314-3772.

Briarwood Park is about half a mile east. Toby Creek Greenway runs a few
hundred yards north.
