Edgehill Park is a Mecklenburg County neighborhood park in Myers Park, and the
document that governs dogs here is the county's parks ordinance. Section 26 runs
one sentence: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of County parks
where pets are expressly prohibited." Dogs are allowed by default. Posted
signage is the only thing that narrows that, and the county publishes nothing
for this park. The /facility/edgehill-park URL returns a 404, and the park is
absent from the department's park list, so nothing site-specific is documented
online either way.

Leash rules for the park aren't published. County animal ordinance Sec. 3-71
requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, which makes it the
operative rule on the grounds.

The shape of the place is a strip, roughly a thousand feet east to west, with
Edgehill Road North along one side and Edgehill Road South along the other and
houses facing in from both. A stream runs the length of it and drops into
culverts at points. A footbridge crosses it near the east end. Section 11 of the
parks ordinance covers that water directly: no bathing dogs "in the streams,
lakes, or other water features of any park."

The walks are short and stepped. Several flights of steps link the paths to
street grade, so the through-route is neither flat nor continuously paved.
Playground at the west end, one tennis court in the middle, a drinking fountain
beside the court, a charcoal grill and benches a few steps from it, a five-space
bike rack. Nothing published says whether that fountain runs, and nothing
addresses water bowls.

No parking lot is mapped inside the property. Parking is on-street, on two
residential streets.

The county's park record leaves the size field blank, so there's no official
acreage; directories give 5.3 acres. The number in that record, (980) 314-1002,
rings the Central Region parks office rather than anything on site. Closing time
is posted at the park and published nowhere online.

Colonial Park sits about half a mile northeast, off Providence Road. Freedom
Park is a bit over a mile southwest.
