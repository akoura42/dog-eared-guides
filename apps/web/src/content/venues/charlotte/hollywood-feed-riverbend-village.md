Hollywood Feed publishes the policy in the company's own words: "Customers are
encouraged to bring leashed dogs in the store, giving employees a chance to
engage and develop a friendship with both the animal and the customer." Dogs go
inside, on the sales floor.

That sentence sits on Hollywood Feed's corporate introduction site, written in
first person by the company on a page aimed at landlords and property managers
rather than shoppers. It's chain-wide. No Charlotte location page repeats it,
and neither does the Facebook page the company runs for this address.

Leashed is the only word the policy uses. No leash length appears anywhere the
chain publishes, which leaves Mecklenburg County's animal ordinance, Sec. 3-71,
as the operative rule: physical restraint anywhere off your own property. Water
bowls aren't mentioned one way or the other.

Which store this is matters, because Charlotte has six. The chain calls this one
Riverbend Village and numbers it 469, and that number does some work. The same
store still appears on the PetPeople site as "Riverbend #469 | PetPeople by
Hollywood Feed," a holdover from the acquisition that brought this location into
the chain. The Facebook page the company runs for the address reads Hollywood
Feed - Riverbend Village.

The self-wash is here. Riverbend Village #469 sits under self wash on the
chain's grooming page, $15.99, no appointment, and the same page shows no
on-staff groomer at this address. Toringdon, on Copper Way, is the Charlotte
store with grooming.

Store hours are a gap. Hollywood Feed's own page for this address doesn't post
them. It prints the phone number and the line "Call for store hours," and the
chain's hours for other Charlotte stores don't carry over.

Riverbend Village sits at Brookshire Boulevard and Mt. Holly-Huntersville Road,
in northwest Charlotte. The center is anchored by an 81,028-square-foot Harris
Teeter that opened in November 2019, at the opposite end of the same lot.
