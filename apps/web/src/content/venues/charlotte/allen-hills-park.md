Allen Hills Park sits off Cheviot Road in Derita, in the county's North
district. Eighteen acres, by Mecklenburg County's own boundary data, classed as
a developed neighborhood park. It has no page on the Park and Recreation site.
The address the county's own records once pointed to now returns a 404, and the
department's park URL for this location is just the site's homepage.

So the county's general rules govern. Section 26 of the Parks & Recreation
Facilities Ordinance reads: "It shall be unlawful to have pets in the areas of
County parks where pets are expressly prohibited." Everywhere else on the
grounds, dogs are allowed. Charlotte-Mecklenburg Animal Care & Control supplies
the leash half, in writing: dogs in public parks not designated as dog parks
must be "on leash and under physical restraint at ALL TIMES."

One gap sits in the middle of that. Park and Recreation's posted trail rules
say "Pets are not allowed in playgrounds or athletic field areas," but that
document covers greenways, not parks generally, and the county doesn't publish
the sign text for this park. Most of the developed acreage here is playground
and ballfield.

What's on the ground, per the county's amenity record: a playground with
swings, two basketball half courts, two ballfields, a multi-purpose field,
picnic area, and restrooms. Half courts, not full ones. The record marks the
park as not ADA compliant and lists parking with a count of five.

There is no dog park here. The county's data marks that field "No," and no
greenway access either, though that last part is changing. The Irwin Creek
Greenway extension runs from Statesville Road to Allen Hills Park by way of
Nevin Park and Ribbonwalk. County project updates put active construction in
Nevin Park, substantial completion in spring 2026 and project completion in
mid-2026. Whether the segment has opened is not posted.
