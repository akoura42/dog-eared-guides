Black Moth Bars pours out of a 1960s camper parked in the Boileryard, the open
courtyard at Camp North End off Statesville Avenue. There's no dining room and
no indoors. The bar is a window in the side of a trailer, and the seating is
whatever the courtyard has out: gliders, fire pits, retro chairs scattered
around. The campus water tower stands over the far end of it.

That setup is why the dog rule here is the landlord's rule. Camp North End's
FAQ puts it plainly: "We're a pet-friendly community. Well-behaved, leashed
dogs are welcome in most outdoor areas (except for the ford green turf) and
even some indoor businesses." The Boileryard is an outdoor area. Ford Green is
the turf lawn on the other side of the campus, and it's the one place the
policy names as off limits.

Black Moth itself publishes nothing about dogs. Not on its site, not on its
Instagram. Water bowls aren't mentioned one way or the other, and neither is
size. The leash requirement comes from the campus policy and from county law.

Two sets of hours are published and they disagree. Camp North End's tenant page
lists the camper open Sunday 2 to 6, Thursday 7 to 10, Friday and Saturday 5 to
10, closed Monday through Wednesday. Black Moth's own Instagram lists Friday
and Saturday, 5 to 10, and nothing else. The rest of the week the business runs
as mobile bartending for weddings and private events, off site.

Parking is free in the surface lot at 1724 Statesville Avenue and free in the
lot at 1801 N. Graham Street. The Razades Way garage runs free for the first
three hours, then $4 an hour. Camp North End's outdoor areas are open to the
public around the clock; the tenants keep their own hours. NoDa Brewing's North
End taproom is about a mile and a half east.

The cocktail list on the site is arranged by season — Lavender Bees Knees and
Southern Rose in spring, Smoked Peach and a Honey-Poblano Margarita in summer.
It's published as the catering menu for booked events. What the camper is
actually pouring on a given night isn't posted anywhere.
