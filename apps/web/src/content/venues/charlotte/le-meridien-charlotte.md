Le Méridien Charlotte publishes its pet terms as numbers rather than
sentiment. Fifty dollars per room, per night, non-refundable. Capped at $150
for the whole stay. Two pets maximum, 80 pounds maximum each.

The cap is the part worth doing arithmetic on. Three nights hits $150 and the
fee stops there. A week costs the same as those three nights.

The hotel sits at 555 South McDowell Street, on the eastern edge of Uptown
where McDowell runs into the Interstate 277 loop. The address carries a tower
designation, North Tower, because two hotels share the building. The Charlotte
Convention Center is two blocks west, which sets the rhythm of the lobby: quiet
weeks, then a few days where check-in lines run out toward the doors.

What the published policy covers is the fee, the weight cap, and the count.
Leash rules for hallways and elevators, water bowls, a dedicated patch of grass
for relief, breed restrictions beyond the weight limit — none of that appears
in the terms the property publishes. Mecklenburg County's animal ordinance,
Sec. 3-71, requires physical restraint anywhere off your own property, so the
sidewalks outside the doors are leashed ground no matter what the hotel says
about its interior.

Walking options within a few minutes are limited to sidewalk. The blocks
immediately around the property are convention-center apron and interstate
shoulder, and shade is scarce on the McDowell side. Little Sugar Creek Greenway
picks up under half a mile southeast, along Kings Drive, with leashed dogs
allowed on the paved path.
