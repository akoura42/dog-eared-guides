Borderline Bar & Billiards is Suite 131 at 4120 Raleigh Street, in the back
corner of The Pass, the warehouse-conversion complex off East Sugar Creek Road
on the northern edge of NoDa. The dog rule lives in the bar's own FAQ and runs
four words: "YES. Dogs are always welcome."

That's the whole of it. No patio-only qualifier, no leash sentence, no weight
cap, and no mention of an outdoor seating area anywhere else on the site
either. The bar serves no food — its FAQ says so plainly, and points guests
toward delivery or whatever they carry in. That matters for the rule that
usually pushes dogs outside in North Carolina, 15A NCAC 18A .2656, which
governs permitted food establishments and their outdoor dining areas. A room
with five pool tables and no kitchen isn't the thing that regulation is aimed
at.

One caveat worth stating. At least one third-party directory describes dogs as
welcome leashed in an outdoor seating area, which is narrower than what
Borderline itself publishes and isn't confirmed by anything official. Leash
specifics and water bowls aren't published one way or the other. County
restraint law applies on the walk in regardless.

Five nine-foot tables, three dart boards, pool free to play, and the bar's
Instagram bio puts the room at 21 and over. Doors at 5 p.m. every day: midnight
close Sunday through Thursday, 2 a.m. Friday and Saturday. Nothing here happens
before dinner.

The Blue Line's Sugar Creek station sits across East Sugar Creek Road from the
development, and the Cross Charlotte Trail runs alongside it, past a mural
under the overpass and a mini-pitch soccer field at the far end. No phone
number is published; the site lists a general manager's email instead.

NoDa Brewing's North End taproom is about a mile and a quarter down North
Tryon.
