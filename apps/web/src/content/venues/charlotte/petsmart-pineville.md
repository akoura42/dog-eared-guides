PetSmart publishes its dog policy rather than leaving it to visitor reports,
and the policy puts dogs on the sales floor. The chain's In-Store Pet Policy
lists domestic dogs among the pets "permitted in PetSmart stores provided the
pets are appropriately secured (leashed or safely confined) and vaccinated (as
appropriate for the particular type of pet)." It applies to every location.
This one is store #2323, at 9515 South Boulevard.

The policy names no leash length. Leashed or safely confined is the whole of
it, which leaves Mecklenburg County's ordinance as the operative restraint
rule: physical restraint anywhere off your own property. Water bowls appear
nowhere, not in the policy and not on the store page.

A Banfield veterinary hospital operates inside this store, and the chain
policy handles vet patients differently from shoppers. Pets arriving for
appointments "must be secured in a crate, kennel, or other fastened enclosure
to prevent exposure to the public," and may not come out "until safely
sheltered inside a hospital examination room or kennel area." Walking in on a
leash is what the policy describes for shopping. Not for an appointment.

The sign says Pineville and the address says Charlotte. PetSmart brands store
#2323 as its Pineville location; the address it publishes is 9515 South
Boulevard, Charlotte 28273, in the Carolina Pavilion center. Pineville itself
is the next town south.

Carolina Pavilion describes itself as "located in South Charlotte adjacent to
I-485 and South Blvd." PetSmart sits mid-run in the strip, with AutoZone at
9509 on one side, Ross and Dollar Tree at 9519 on the other, and Target across
the lot at 9531. The parking is the center's shared surface lot, which the
22-screen AMC draws on as well.

Services listed on the store page: grooming, dog training, the Banfield,
adoptions, and curbside pickup. No PetsHotel and no Doggie Day Camp appear on
it, which separates this store from the Cotswold PetSmart about seven miles
northeast, where boarding and day camp run out of the same building.

Hours as posted: 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Monday through Saturday, 10 to 7 on Sunday.
Curbside pickup runs daily from open until 7. Everything but the Banfield
closes with the store at 8; the Banfield closes at 6.

Archdale Animal Hospital is about three miles north, off South Boulevard.
