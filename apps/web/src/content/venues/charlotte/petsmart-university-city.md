PetSmart's dog policy is published rather than reported, and it puts dogs on
the sales floor. The chain's In-Store Pet Policy names domestic dogs among the
pets "permitted in PetSmart stores provided the pets are appropriately secured
(leashed or safely confined) and vaccinated (as appropriate for the particular
type of pet)." It applies to every location. This one is store #2347, the
Charlotte University store, in Suite 25 at 8150 Ikea Boulevard.

Leash length isn't specified. Leashed or safely confined is the whole of the
requirement, which leaves Mecklenburg County's ordinance as the operative rule:
physical restraint anywhere off your own property. Water bowls go unmentioned,
on the policy page and the store page both.

One condition that applies at some PetSmarts doesn't reach this one. The crate
rule in the chain policy governs pets arriving for appointments at in-store
veterinary hospitals, and the page for #2347 lists no hospital. What it does
list runs short: grooming, training, adoptions, curbside pickup. No PetsHotel,
no Doggie Day Camp, no vet clinic. This is a retail floor with a grooming salon
and a training ring, not one of the large-format stores.

Hours as posted: 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. Monday through Saturday, 10 to 7 on Sunday.
Grooming and training run until 9, adoptions until 7, and curbside pickup
daily from open until 7.

The suite sits in the Belgate center, the strip of big-box tenants that share
one surface lot off Ikea Boulevard. Marshalls, Old Navy, World Market and Hobby
Lobby all carry the same 8150 street address in their own store directories.
IKEA anchors the far end of the boulevard it named.

Briarwood Park is the nearest published green space, about three miles south
off the same side of town.
