Briarwood Park is a Mecklenburg County park at 5349 Waterwood Street in east
Charlotte, which means the county's rules are the park's rules. The Parks and
Recreation Facilities Ordinance states it in one line: it is unlawful "to have
pets in the areas of County parks where pets are expressly prohibited."
Everywhere else on the grounds, dogs are allowed. Leashed.

Where they aren't allowed is most of what's built here. Park and Recreation's
own posted rules put the leash at "6 ft. or shorter," tell owners to pick up
after a pet, and state that "Pets are not allowed in playgrounds or athletic
field areas." Briarwood is two ball diamonds and a playground. Subtract those
and what's left is the mown ground between them, the concrete walkways, and the
asphalt.

There is no county web page for this park. It appears in the county's own GIS
as a developed community park in the North region, at the Waterwood Street
address, and that's the extent of what the agency publishes about it. Hours are
the same story. Section 31 of the ordinance makes it unlawful to be in a park
after "the posted closing time for that park," so the sign at the entrance is
the only place that time exists.

Water fountains and bowls: the county doesn't address them here one way or the
other. Section 11 of the ordinance does bar bathing dogs in "the streams,
lakes, or other water features of any park."

Off-leash isn't legal on these grounds. Mecklenburg County's fenced dog parks
are the only off-leash ground in the system, and the east-side one is
Barkingham Park at Reedy Creek Park, 2900 Rocky River Road, open daily from
7:30 a.m. to sunset with separate large- and small-dog sections. Rabies with
tag number, DHLPP, and Bordetella vaccinations are required to enter it.

Parking is several small asphalt lots off Waterwood. Briarwood Academy, the
elementary school, sits next door.
