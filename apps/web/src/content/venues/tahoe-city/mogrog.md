MOGROG publishes nothing about dogs. It's a rotisserie built into a 1973
Unimog, so where a dog can stand depends entirely on whose ground the truck is
parked on. Their site names two places.

In winter the truck becomes MOGROG Cafe at Alpine Meadows, set next to Achieve
Tahoe at the bottom of the Subway chair, close to the parking lot at 2680
Alpine Meadows Road. That ground belongs to Palisades Tahoe, which publishes
the rule: "Leashed dogs are permitted in The Village at Palisades Tahoe and our
parking lots year-round." Two limits land near the truck. Dogs are barred from
the sundeck and breezeway of the Alpine main lodge, the closest sit-down space
to the window. Pets are also barred from ski runs and chairlifts during the
winter season, backpacks included. The resort's affirmative permission names
the Village and the parking lots specifically; it doesn't describe the strip of
base area between the lot and the lift, which is where the truck actually sits.

Service is through a window. The FAQ describes ordering and asking for
modifications there. The site doesn't describe seating of any kind, and it
doesn't mention water bowls one way or the other.

The summer address is Commons Beach, and dogs are not allowed on that beach.
TCPUD's rule reads: "No dogs are allowed on Commons Beach, except for leashed
dogs on the paved multi-use trail." The Tahoe City Farmers Market runs in the
same footprint on Thursday mornings, and MOGROG lists itself among the trucks
there. The market's own rule: "Pets aren't allowed in the stall area, but you
can use our 'Doggy Jail,' located next to the old jail by the entrance. Simply
leash your pup to the provided slackline; shade and water are available." That
puts a leashed dog in shade at the entrance while someone else walks the stalls.

No phone number appears on the site. Contact runs through info@mogrog.com. No
menu is published either; the FAQ says the menu rotates by season and the
ingredients change week to week.
