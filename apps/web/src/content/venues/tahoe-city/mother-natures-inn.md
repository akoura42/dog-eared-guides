Mother Nature's Inn doesn't just tolerate dogs — it calls itself "North Lake
Tahoe's Pet Friendly Inn" on its own homepage, which is about as clear a
signal as lodging gives. This is the budget-friendly counterpoint to the
lake's resort pricing: a classic-Tahoe, cabin-style motor inn with simple,
clean rooms and knotty-pine character, sitting right on North Lake Boulevard
between the lake and the Tahoe City Golf Course.

The pet terms, from the inn's published policies: a $75 fee per stay (not per
night), a maximum of two pets per room, pets under control at all times,
cleaned up after, and any damage billed to you. One practical wrinkle — only
certain rooms are pet-eligible, and the inn's own room listings mark which
("Rm. 3, 2 Doubles, pet friendly," for example). Book a designated pet room
rather than assuming.

For a two-dog household, note that this is one of the few verified two-pet
policies in town. On a one- or two-night stay the $75 flat fee is steeper per
night than Basecamp's $40, but for a week it works out cheaper — worth doing
the math for longer trips.

The location is the real amenity. You're steps from Tahoe City's shops, pubs,
and eateries, and right across from the lakefront — remembering that Commons
Beach itself is a no-dog zone, so your morning walk goes along the paved
multi-use trail instead (leashed dogs welcome). Palisades Tahoe, Alpine
Meadows, and Homewood are all minutes away by car, making this a workable
winter base if one of your party skis while the other walks the dog on the
plowed sections of trail.

The policy doesn't say whether pets can be left unattended in rooms — call
ahead if your plans depend on it.
