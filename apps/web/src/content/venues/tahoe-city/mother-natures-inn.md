Mother Nature's Inn calls itself "North Lake Tahoe's Pet Friendly Inn" on its
own homepage. It's a cabin-style motor inn on North Lake Boulevard between
the lake and the Tahoe City Golf Course — simple, clean rooms with
knotty-pine character, priced at the budget end of a lake that mostly runs
resort rates.

The pet terms come from the inn's published policies. The fee is $75 per
stay, not per night. Two pets per room, maximum, which makes this one of the
few verified two-pet policies in town. Pets stay under control at all times
and cleaned up after, and any damage gets billed to the guest. Only certain
rooms take pets; the inn's own room listings mark which ones ("Rm. 3, 2
Doubles, pet friendly," for example). For comparison, Basecamp down the
street charges a $40 one-time fee, so the $75 here runs higher over a night
or two and lower over a week.

The inn sits steps from Tahoe City's shops and pubs, directly across from
the lakefront. Commons Beach, the sand across the way, doesn't allow dogs.
The paved multi-use trail along the water does, leashed, and sections of it
are plowed in winter. Palisades Tahoe and Alpine Meadows are minutes away by
car; so is Homewood.

The policy doesn't say whether pets can be left unattended in rooms. The
inn's number is (530) 581-4278.
