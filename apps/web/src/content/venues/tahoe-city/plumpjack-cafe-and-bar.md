PlumpJack Cafe and Bar occupies the ground floor of the PlumpJack Inn at 1920
Olympic Valley Road, across the road from the tram building at the base of
Palisades Tahoe. The cafe publishes no dog policy. Neither does the inn: its
pet page and FAQ cover dogs in guest rooms, at a one-time $150 for one dog and
$200 for two, and stop there. This listing rests on outside reports.

Three of them, and they agree on the patio. Palisades Tahoe's pet page, in the
paragraph recommending the inn as the valley's dog-friendly lodging, says
"Dogs on leash are also welcome to accompany owners while dining on the
outdoor patio." DogTrekker's April 2023 entry describes the same patio and
adds water bowls and treats. A TripAdvisor review from August 2014 reports
dogs on the patio plus one dog-friendly table inside the bar. That indoor
table appears nowhere else in twelve years.

The patio tracks the kitchen calendar. Summer lunch runs Saturday and Sunday,
noon to 3 pm, in the bar and on the patio, weather permitting. Dinner is
Thursday through Monday, 5 pm to 9 pm, with a limited late-night menu Thursday
and Friday until 10. The cafe's site and the inn's FAQ disagree on when the
bar opens, 4 pm on one and 3 pm on the other. Select dates close for private
events.

The inn sits outside the Village proper, a few minutes' walk up Olympic Valley
Road from it. Leashed dogs are permitted to walk through the Village and the
resort parking lots year-round under Palisades Tahoe's own pet policy, which
is a separate question from table service at any Village restaurant. No leash
rule is published for this patio by the cafe or the inn; Placer County's leash
ordinance covers the public ground around it. The water bowls and treats are a
directory claim, not a published amenity.
