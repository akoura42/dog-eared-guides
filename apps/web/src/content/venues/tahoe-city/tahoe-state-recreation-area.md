Tahoe State Recreation Area is a 23-site campground on Highway 28, a quarter
mile east of downtown Tahoe City. The Lakeside loop runs between the highway
and the water. The Hillside loop sits across Highway 28 from both.

The dog rules arrive in two layers that don't quite meet. State Parks' page
for this unit names one dog-allowed area: the "Truckee River Outlet known as
'Gatekeepers' within the day-use area along the Lake Tahoe shoreline." That
carve-out is a separate spot half a mile west, at the Gatekeepers day-use
area. The department's statewide dog page, meanwhile, allows dogs in
campgrounds and day-use areas on a six-foot leash, and carries a rule that
only makes sense for camping: overnight campers must keep their dog in a
tent or vehicle. Both pages are official. Neither says plainly whether a
leashed dog can occupy a campsite here.

What both agree on: leashed at all times, six feet maximum, never
unattended. And off the beach. California State Parks closes its beaches to
dogs unless a beach is posted open, and the shoreline along this campground
carries no such posting.

Sites come with picnic tables, fire pits, and required-use bear lockers.
Showers and restrooms are for registered campers. No hookups, no dump
station. Trailers cap at 15 feet, RVs at 21, and the parking pads are short.
Two vehicles per site; a third costs $10.

The park page states "NO DAY USE AT THE CAMPGROUND" in those words, so the
lot is for registered campers.

A paved bike path links the campground to town, and leashed dogs are allowed
across that trail network the whole way to 64-Acres Park at its far end. The
official page doesn't mention water bowls or any pet fee one way or the
other.
