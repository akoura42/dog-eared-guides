Le Chamois publishes no dog policy. The site covers hours, the location, and a
full menu, and it never mentions dogs, pets, or even the outdoor bar. This
listing rests on visitor reports rather than anything the venue has stated.

Two TripAdvisor reviewers, twenty months apart, put dogs in the outdoor
drinking area. A March 2015 review calls the place dog friendly in the same
breath as the outside bar and the kid playground next door. A November 2016
review describes "families, skids, dogs, mountain heads and locals all sitting
on benches and rocks sharing a beer." Neither addresses the indoor rooms or the
upstairs Loft Bar, and neither does the venue.

The ground around the building does carry an official rule. Palisades Tahoe's
pets page says leashed dogs are permitted in The Village at Palisades Tahoe and
its parking lots year-round, and that dogs stay leashed in public areas of the
base area. That rule stops at the door of any individual business. The only
restaurant patio the resort names on that page is PlumpJack Inn's.

The Chammy runs winter only. As of August 2026 the front page reads "We are now
closed for the season. Enjoy your summer! See you in December," with winter
hours listed as 11am to 7pm or later, daily. Both dog reports come from ski
season, when the outdoor scene is snow and benches.

The address is 1960 Olympic Valley Road, at the mountain base about nine miles
northwest of Tahoe City. The paved Truckee River Trail covers most of that
distance from 64-Acres Park and takes leashed dogs. Food is pizza, spaghetti,
chili, and hot sandwiches, ordered inside. Leash rules for the venue's own
outdoor space aren't published, so Placer County's leash law is what applies,
and nothing on the site or in the reports mentions water bowls one way or the
other.
