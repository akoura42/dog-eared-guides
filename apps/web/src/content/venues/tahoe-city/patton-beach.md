Dogs are allowed on part of this beach, not all of it. The rule from the
California Tahoe Conservancy, which owns the land, is that pets are permitted
only when leashed, only on the eastern portion of the beach, and on the deck of
the café. Everything west of that is off limits to dogs. A mutt mitt dispenser
sits near the parking lot.

Three acres of pebble beach at 5166 North Lake Boulevard, directly east of
Sierra Boat Company, about six miles northeast of Tahoe City on Highway 28.
Tahoe Waterman holds the concession and runs it year-round: a café with
breakfast, lunch, coffee, beer and wine, plus rentals of stand-up paddleboards,
kayaks, canoes, and outrigger canoes. The café deck is the one spot on the
property where a dog can sit while someone eats. The car-top launch for
nonmotorized craft is free.

Parking is the pinch point. Twenty-three spaces, one of them accessible, most
of them within 500 feet of the water. Restrooms, picnic tables, benches,
barbecues, and shade are on site, with paved pathways three to five feet wide
and a short boardwalk down to the beach.

Footing is rounded stone rather than sand, which is what the seasonal mobility
mats exist to solve. Neither the Conservancy's page nor Tahoe Waterman's site
marks where the eastern portion begins, and neither mentions water bowls one way
or the other.

Compared with the Tahoe City beaches, this one trades size for a shorter dog
list of exceptions: Lake Tahoe Commons Beach bans dogs outright, and Skylandia
Park and Lake Forest Beach take leashed dogs across the whole site. Patton
splits the difference by geography. The café deck is the part that has no
equivalent in town.
