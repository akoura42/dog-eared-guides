Fire Sign Cafe publishes no dog policy. Not on its website, not in its
Instagram bio, not on any page the cafe controls. This listing rests on
visitor-facing reports rather than anything the restaurant has stated.

Two independent Lake Tahoe directories agree on the shape of it. Tahoe
Getaways' dining guide lists a "dog friendly patio" and notes outside
seating in the summer. Tahoe Rental Company's writeup says more: trained
service dogs only inside the restaurant, with smaller dogs allowed on the
patio during the months it's open. That second source is the only one
carrying the smaller-dogs qualifier and the service-animal line. The cafe
hasn't confirmed either.

The practical read is that the patio is the whole story here. Indoor
seating isn't reported as an option for pets by either source, and the
patio is seasonal, so the reported dog access tracks the summer calendar.

The address is 1785 West Lake Boulevard, which is Highway 89 at that
point. The cafe sits about two miles south of the Y on the West Shore run,
close to Sunnyside Restaurant and Lodge. Highway traffic passes the
frontage, and the lot is the only parking. The cafe also runs The Annex, a
separate to-go counter for coffee, tea, cocktails and breakfast items.

Leash rules are not published by the venue. Placer County leash law
applies. The cafe says nothing about water bowls one way or the other.
Hours run breakfast through early afternoon, seven days, with the kitchen
closing mid-afternoon.

The menus on the official site are posted as flat images and a PDF rather
than readable text, so no dish list is captured here. Anyone routing a dog
walk around a meal has the Truckee River Trail and the 64-Acres trailhead
a few minutes north, both of which take leashed dogs.
