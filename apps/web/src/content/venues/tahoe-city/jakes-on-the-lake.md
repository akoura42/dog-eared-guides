Jake's on the Lake occupies the water side of the Boatworks building at 780
North Lake Boulevard, at the eastern end of downtown Tahoe City. Two outdoor
decks face the lake, an upper and a lower. The lower one is covered, and it's
the one that turns up in every dog report.

Jake's publishes no dog policy. Nothing on jakestahoe.com, nothing on the
restaurant's Facebook page, and the Instagram account isn't readable without an
account. This listing rests on visitor reports instead, which is thinner ground
than most pages here. Tahoe Tap Haus, a few minutes west on the same boulevard,
states its patio policy on its own site.

What the reports agree on: dogs on the lower covered patio, at lunch. Two
visitors describe eating out there with a dog, one of them noting shade and a
water view from the patio corner. Two others describe dogs not being allowed on
the deck at dinner. A dog-travel directory carries the restriction in a single
line, lunch only, and a North Lake Tahoe roundup lists Jake's as a place to eat
outdoors with a dog without mentioning hours at all. One reviewer contradicts
all of it and says the restaurant admits certified service animals only. Nothing
published by the restaurant settles that.

Lunch is a narrow window. The restaurant's own hours put it at Friday through
Sunday, 11:30 to 3, with dinner running every day from 5 to 9. The reported dog
window is those three afternoons. The cafe menu covers that service: Jake's
Burger, filet tacos, fish tacos, a poké bowl, clam chowder, and the same crab
cakes and calamari that open the dinner menu.

Nothing is published about leashing, water bowls, a pet fee, or size limits, and
no visitor report mentions any of them either. The upper deck appears in no dog
report at all.
