Basecamp Tahoe City is the rare hotel whose pet policy reads like it was
written by someone who actually likes dogs. The FAQ opens with "Furry friends
are our favorite!" — and the substance backs it up: dogs are allowed not just
in designated rooms but throughout the property's common areas, which changes
the whole feel of a stay. You're not smuggling a dog past the lobby; you're
hanging out by the fire pit with it.

The terms, verified against the hotel's own FAQ: dogs stay in select rooms
(ask for a pet-eligible room when you book — not every room qualifies),
leashed and under control in shared spaces, with a one-time fee of $40 plus
tax per visit rather than a per-night charge. On a multi-night stay that flat
fee undercuts most per-night pet pricing in the basin.

The one rule that shapes your trip: **pets cannot be left alone in the room,
ever.** That's not a suggestion — it's in the policy. Plan Tahoe City
accordingly, which is honestly easy: the paved lakefront trail takes leashed
dogs, the Tahoe Tap Haus patio is a short walk down North Lake Boulevard, and
Skylandia Park's woodland trails are a few minutes east. Dinner somewhere
without a patio, though, means someone stays back with the dog.

Location-wise you're on the main drag at the north end of town — walkable to
shops and the lakefront, a quick drive to the Truckee River Trail trailhead at
64-Acres Park. For dog owners the combination of flat fee, common-area
access, and in-town walkability makes this the default recommendation in
Tahoe City proper.
