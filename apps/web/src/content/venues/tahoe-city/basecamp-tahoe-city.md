Basecamp Tahoe City sits on North Lake Boulevard at the north end of town,
within walking distance of the shops and the lakefront and a short drive from
the Truckee River Trail trailhead at 64-Acres Park.

The pet policy comes from the hotel's own FAQ, which opens with "Furry
friends are our favorite!" Dogs stay in select rooms (not every room
qualifies; pet-eligible rooms are requested at booking) and are allowed
throughout the common areas, leashed and under control. That includes the
fire pit. The fee is $40 plus tax, charged once per visit rather than per
night, and on a multi-night stay that flat structure runs cheaper than most
per-night pet pricing around the lake.

One line in the policy carries the most weight: pets may not be left alone in
the room at any point during a stay. That rule interacts with the rest of
town in a specific way. The paved lakefront trail takes leashed dogs. The
Tahoe Tap Haus patio, a short walk down North Lake Boulevard, does too, and
Skylandia Park's woodland trails are a few minutes east. Restaurants without
patios are the gap — the no-alone rule doesn't pause for dinner.

The FAQ doesn't mention water bowls one way or the other.
