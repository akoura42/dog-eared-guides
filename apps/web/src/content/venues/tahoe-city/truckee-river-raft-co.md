Truckee River Raft Company runs the classic Tahoe City float: put in near
Fanny Bridge where the lake empties into the river, drift five miles of
mostly flat water, take out at River Ranch Lodge. Self-guided, about two
to three hours. The company describes the run as mild and easy to
navigate, with a few surprise rapids.

On dogs, the company's homepage says it plainly: "We happily welcome dogs
and encourage you to bring lunch and drinks." That's the whole published
policy — no leash rule, size limit, or dog fee appears anywhere on the
site, and flotation for dogs isn't addressed one way or the other.

The take-out doubles as lunch. The float ends at River Ranch Lodge, whose
deck over the river rapids allows leashed dogs — it's a verified listing
in this guide — and the shuttle bus runs the return trip to the start,
8:30am to 6pm.

The season runs Memorial Day weekend through Labor Day. Adults (13 and
up) are $77, children 6–12 are $40. The office sits at 185 River Road
just south of the "Y", with customer parking published at 2200 River
Road, and the paved trail from 64-Acres Park reaches the put-in on foot.
