River Ranch is the payoff at the end of the best dog walk in Tahoe City.
The lodge's deck hangs over a bend of the Truckee River at the bottom of
the Alpine Meadows road, close enough to the rapids that rafters pull out
right below you — and the lodge's own site says what most Tahoe restaurants
won't put in writing: "Leashed Dogs Welcome on Outside Patio."

The move that makes this special: don't drive. River Ranch sits directly on
the Truckee River Trail, about four miles downstream from the 64-Acres
trailhead in Tahoe City. Walk or bike the paved riverside path with the dog
(short leash, per TCPUD's trail rules), arrive with everyone properly
exercised, and take a patio table. The patio bar and lunch service is
counter-style with open seating — no reservation dance, well suited to a
party that includes a damp dog. Dinner (5–9pm nightly, reservations
recommended) runs inside and out, but dogs stay patio-side.

Timing notes from the lodge's own announcements: the deck is the summer
engine of this place and occasionally closes for private events, so check
their site before making it the centerpiece of your day. In rafting season
the scene is genuinely fun — the river is the entertainment — but it also
means peak crowds on July weekends; the trail-plus-late-lunch combination
beats arriving at noon.

One verification footnote we owe you: River Ranch's website blocks
automated readers, so we confirmed the dog policy against an archived
capture of the lodge's own site rather than the live page. Same words,
their site. The lodge also rents rooms, and third-party directories claim a
pet policy for lodging — but the official site doesn't publish one we could
verify, so we're listing the restaurant only. If you want to sleep here
with the dog, call first.
