River Ranch's deck hangs over a bend of the Truckee River at the bottom
of the Alpine Meadows road, close enough to the rapids that rafters pull
out directly below the rail. And the lodge's own site puts the dog policy
in writing: "Leashed Dogs Welcome on Outside Patio."

The lodge sits directly on the Truckee River Trail, about four miles
downstream from the 64-Acres trailhead in Tahoe City — a paved riverside
path where TCPUD's rules require a short leash. The patio bar and lunch
service runs counter-style with open seating, no reservations. Dinner is
5–9pm nightly, inside and out, with reservations recommended by the
lodge. Dogs stay patio-side.

The deck is the summer engine of the place, and by the lodge's own
announcements it occasionally closes for private events. In rafting
season the river is the entertainment, boats pulling out below the deck
all afternoon. July weekends bring the peak crowds.

One verification footnote: River Ranch's website blocks automated
readers, so the dog policy was confirmed against an archived capture of

The lodge also rents rooms, and third-party directories claim a lodging
pet policy, but the official site doesn't publish one that could be

stay the night is a question only the lodge answers.
