Truckee-Tahoe Pet Lodge is the closest full-service boarding and daycare
operation to Tahoe City, on West River Street in Truckee. It has run
since 2010, and the services list covers the can't-bring-the-dog
scenarios: all-suite overnight boarding, daycare by the day, and a
grooming and spa side.

Hours run seven days: 7:30am to 6pm Monday through Thursday and on
weekends, with Friday open until 7:30pm. New customers book through a
reservation-request process on the website rather than walk-in — the
site doesn't publish how much lead time a summer weekend needs.

The drive from Tahoe City runs up Highway 89 along the river, the same
road as the raft take-outs. Vaccination requirements aren't published on
the main site; the reservation process is where those details surface.
