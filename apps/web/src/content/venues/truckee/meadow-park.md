Truckee has no general leash law, and Meadow Park isn't one of the exceptions.

The town's animal code sets a control standard instead of a leash requirement.
Section 8.01.020(f) defines "at large" as an animal "off the premises of its
owner and not under the control of the owner by visual and voice control or by
means of a leash, chain, tether, or adequate fence." Section 8.01.420 then makes
it unlawful to let an animal be at large "in or upon any public street,
sidewalk, park, school ground, or other public place." A leash satisfies that.
So does demonstrated voice and visual control.

The code names exactly one venue. Subsection (d) requires a leash in every part
of Truckee River Regional Park from May 1 through October 31, and that park is
about two miles east. The seasonal rule stops at its boundary.

Truckee-Donner Recreation & Park District runs Meadow Park and publishes no dog
rule of its own on the facility page. Nothing about drinking water, nothing
about waste stations, nothing about which parts of the grounds are open to dogs.

What's here is 2.5 acres of natural grass split into an Upper Field and a Lower
Field, both with skinned infields, grass outfields, and home and visitor
dugouts. The Lower Field carries a score shed. Around them sit a picnic area,
restrooms, and a playground. The district lists the fields as hosting youth
soccer, baseball, and football practices and games.

The park sits a block off Donner Pass Road, behind the Truckee Donner PUD
building at 11570 Donner Pass Road. The way in is Donner Trail Road, a
residential street posted at 25 mph. Truckee High School's track and football
field are a few hundred yards off. Donner Pass Road is the nearest through
route, 35 mph with a bike lane, and it's where the traffic noise comes from.

There's no water on the grounds. The nearest dog-legal shoreline is East Beach
at Donner Memorial State Park, roughly two miles west, where California State
Parks requires a six-foot leash and the town's voice-control standard doesn't
apply.

Park hours aren't posted online. The number above rings the district office on
Truckee Way, not anything at the park.
