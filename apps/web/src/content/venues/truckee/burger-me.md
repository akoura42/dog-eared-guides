Burger Me publishes no dog policy. Its site runs seven pages, counting both
menus and the accessibility notice, and not one of them says anything about
dogs, pets, or the patio. The Facebook page is silent too, as is the Truckee
Chamber listing. This entry rests on visitor reporting, not on the restaurant.

Two independent sources describe the same arrangement. DogTrekker's listing for
the Truckee store: "What makes Burger Me work for eating with your dog is their
outdoor seating area." Tahoe Truckee Vacations, in a roundup of dog-friendly
North Lake Tahoe and Truckee places, calls it "casual picnic-table seating" and
tells readers to bring their pup. Outside, in both accounts. Neither describes
dogs indoors, and neither is the restaurant.

What no source covers is everything after that. Leash rules, water bowls, size
or breed limits, a fee. None of it is published, by the venue or by anyone
reporting on it. Truckee's animal ordinance governs the sidewalk out front
regardless of what the restaurant does or doesn't say.

The address is 10418 Donner Pass Road, Suite A. That's roughly half a mile west
of Commercial Row and the railroad tracks, fronting Donner Pass Road itself
rather than a downtown side street. Hours as posted on the restaurant's own
locations page: eleven to nine, seven days a week. Truckee is one of five Burger
Me locations, alongside two in Reno, one in South Lake Tahoe, and one in Auburn.

The food menu splits in two. Build Your Own covers beef, a house-made turkey
patty, free-range chicken grilled or fried, bison, elk, an organic veggie patty,
and a quarter-pound all-beef hot dog. Favorites runs longer: Patty Melt, Fish
Sando, Dirty Bird, Big Dippa, a lamb Gyro Burger, BBQ Bison, Ahi, Cowboy, NV
Elk, Buffalo Chicken Sando, Smash Burger. Sides are fries three ways, onion
rings, and house-made turkey chili. Two salads, a kids list, hand-spun shakes
and malts.

Alibi Ale Works' Truckee Public House, which does put its dog rule in writing on
its own site, is about ten minutes on foot east across the tracks.
