Spoiled Petz is a grooming salon, so the dog policy is the business model:
dogs come inside, on their own appointment. The site sets one condition for
walking in without one. "For walk in nail trims we do require proof of
rabies." Nothing published addresses leashes in the lobby or water bowls, and
Truckee's animal ordinance governs the lot and the sidewalk regardless.

What happens after drop-off is published plainly. "We do kennel dogs in
between their grooms, we don't allow free roaming," which the salon explains
as keeping different families' dogs from mingling. Muzzles come out only for
nail trims, and only if necessary, never during a bath or in the kennel.
Sedated dogs are turned away without a prescription from a vet. Matted coats
past what grooming can fix get referred to a veterinarian. Turnaround runs two
to six hours depending on breed.

This was Scraps. The Facebook page still leads with it: "Formally known as
Scraps! Same owners and Groomers." Scraps Pet Store & Dog Bakery sat at 12030
Donner Pass Road, and a 2014 Sierra Sun profile described treats baked
in-house and a fifteen-dollar self-wash station there. The salon is now on
Deerfield Drive in Gateway, off the west end of Donner Pass Road near the
freeway interchange, about a mile and a half east of the Donner Memorial State
Park entrance. Whether the retail side made the move isn't published anywhere.
The Facebook page still files the business under Pet Store; the website
describes grooming and nothing else.

Two addresses circulate. The website says 11668 Deerfield Drive, Suite C-1;
both Facebook pages and every directory say 11448, same suite. The suite
number is the only part all of them agree on.

Hours as posted: "Monday - Sunday 9am- until last dog is Complete," with the
site noting that hours and days vary by location. Online booking runs through
groomer.io and recognizes returning customers only; new ones complete an
intake form first. The salon lists text at (775) 800-7005 as the fastest
contact and says calls are returned within twenty-four business hours.
