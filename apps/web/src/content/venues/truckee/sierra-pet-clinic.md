Sierra Pet Clinic is a veterinary hospital, which settles the dog question in
one direction: dogs go inside, because dogs are the patients. The clinic's own
site says it is "a full-service hospital that provides care to dogs and cats,"
and Cat and Dog Care heads the service list. What the site doesn't publish is
any condition on entry. No leash line, no carrier rule, no proof of vaccination
at the door. Truckee's animal control ordinance covers the street outside
regardless of what the clinic does or doesn't post.

The practice has been at this since 1985. Three veterinarians are listed: Megan
Arce, Stephanie Foreman, and Abigail Mitchell. The published services run to
wellness exams, dental care, spay and neuter, soft tissue and orthopedic
surgery, x-rays and ultrasound, on-site lab work, microchipping, laser therapy,
ophthalmology, an online pharmacy, and end-of-life care. No boarding, no
grooming, no daycare. That absence is why no vaccination requirement appears
anywhere on the site.

Hours are eight to five, Monday through Friday. Closed Saturday and Sunday, and
closed New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving,
and Christmas. For storm closures and power or phone outages, the clinic points
people to its Facebook page, which at this elevation is a live category of
closure rather than a formality.

Emergencies during business hours are for current patients only, and the site is
direct about the mechanics: "Please call us prior to your arrival so that we may
prepare for you and your pet." Outside those hours the clinic names two Reno
hospitals — Animal Emergency and Specialty Center at (775) 851-3600, and Blue
Pearl Pet Hospital at (775) 358-8555. Neither is in Truckee.

The address is 10411 River Park Place, off Donner Pass Road on the river side of
town, a quarter mile from Burger Me and a bit over half a mile west of
Commercial Row. The location page lists a service area well past the town line:
Tahoe City, Carnelian Bay, Olympic Valley, Kings Beach, Incline Village, and
Reno.
