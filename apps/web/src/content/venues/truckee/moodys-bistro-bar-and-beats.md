Moody's publishes no dog policy anywhere. Not on moodysbistro.com, not on its
Facebook or Instagram accounts, and not on the site of the Truckee Hotel, whose
ground floor it has occupied since 2002. What follows rests on outside listings.

Three of them, agreeing on one thing: dogs at the outdoor tables. Visit Truckee
Tahoe, the regional tourism bureau, puts Moody's on its dog-friendly roundup and
says visitors can dine with a dog on the pet-friendly patio. DogTrekker's
listing, from July 2022, describes a shaded patio facing the downtown sidewalks
whose outdoor dining area "welcomes leashed dogs." A TrustedHousesitters guide
from November 2025 says of the outdoor tables that "furry friends are welcome."
None of the three is the restaurant. None says anything about the interior, so
the listing here stops at the patio.

Nothing published covers water bowls, a fee, or a size limit. No leash rule
comes from the restaurant either, which leaves the Town of Truckee's animal code
as the standard downtown. Title 8 prohibits dogs at large but accepts visual and
voice control, defined as the owner being in the immediate presence of the
animal and able to demonstrate effective control.

The building is the 1873 Truckee Hotel at 10007 Bridge Street, four stories of
restored Italianate in the middle of historic downtown. Upstairs is off the
table entirely: the hotel's own site states "no pets allowed" for its thirty-six
rooms. Street level is where the outdoor seating sits. The railroad tracks and
the Amtrak depot are about two blocks north, and freight moves through at all
hours.

The restaurant's about page calls the outdoor space a summer patio seating
fifty, and says "In summer dine alfresco surrounded by flowers." Whether it
opens outside summer isn't published. Live music runs Thursday through Saturday
in the bar, which is indoors: jazz, Americana, soul, R&B, with a splash of hip
hop. Kitchen hours are 11:30 a.m. to close daily, under executive chef William
Burns. The summer menus run brick-oven pizzas, ahi poke, duck liver paté,
house-made strozzapreti and mafaldine, a lamb rack, and a Niman Ranch
cheeseburger.

A block up Bridge Street, Alibi Ale Works' Truckee Public House states its rule
outright on its own site: leashed dogs in the heated beer garden, never indoors.
