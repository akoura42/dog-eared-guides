Locals call this Dog Beach. California State Parks calls it East Beach, and
that is the name the rule attaches to.

District Superintendent's Order 683-22-002, signed in May 2022, lists it among
four places in the Sierra District where dogs are an outright exception to the
statewide ban in Title 14, Section 4312(a): "Donner Memorial SP known as 'East
Beach,' within the day use area on the eastern shore of Donner Lake within 200
yards of the Tahoe-Donner Boat Launching Facility." Dogs are allowed there on a
leash. The park's own info sheet sets the length at six feet, maximum. No order
designates an off-leash area anywhere on Donner Lake. The nickname is local
usage, not a legal status.

The beach sits at the east end of the lake, within a couple hundred yards of
the Tahoe Donner Beach Club Marina, which is private and members-only. Tahoe
Donner publishes the seam between the two: dogs aren't permitted at the marina
unless they're service animals, and the stated exception is entering the state
park from the marina parking lot, leashed.

Elsewhere on the lake the answer changes. China Cove Beach, the park's other
swim beach, is the one stretch of shore where the park excludes dogs outright.
West End Beach, run by the recreation district at the far end, admits none
either.

Donner Lake sits at 5,914 feet. I-80 runs along the north side and passes
within a few hundred yards of the water at this end, so traffic noise carries
across the beach through the day. Restrooms in the day-use areas are open in
summer only. The park publishes nothing about drinking water at the beach
itself.

Truckee's town rule, which accepts immediate voice and visual control in place
of a leash, stops at the park boundary. Inside, it's six feet of leash,
year-round. The district order exempts trained service dogs.
