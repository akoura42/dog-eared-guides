Dogs aren't allowed at The Truckee Hotel. The hotel's own site states it in a
single line on the rooms page, filed with the smoking rule: "100% non-smoking,
no pets allowed." The same sentence appears on the home and about pages.
Service animals aren't mentioned anywhere on the site, so no carve-out is
published either way.

Behind the ban is a building from 1873. It opened as the American House, a
stagecoach stop on the Dutch Flat Wagon Road, became the Whitney House, and
took its current name in 1976. The four-story Italianate exterior was restored
in 1992. Thirty-six rooms: twenty-eight European rooms with in-room sinks and
baths down the hall, eight American rooms with private baths and claw-foot
tubs. Four family rooms sleep up to five. Published rates run $79 to $229.
Continental breakfast is 7 to 10 a.m., wi-fi is complimentary.

Moody's Bistro, Bar & Beats occupies the ground floor. The hotel links to it
and publishes no dog policy for the restaurant or its street-level seating.

The address is 10007 Bridge Street, in the middle of historic downtown. The
Amtrak depot at 10065 Donner Pass Road sits about two blocks away, where the
California Zephyr stops twice daily.

Nearest lodging that publishes a dog policy: Gravity Haus Truckee-Tahoe, 10918
Brockway Road, roughly two miles southeast, formerly the Cedar House Sport
Hotel. Its site lists dog-ready rooms and says pet fees "do apply & can be paid
for at booking," waived for members. It doesn't publish the fee amount, breed
limits, or a weight cap.

Outside, downtown runs on the Town's own rule. Truckee doesn't require a leash.
Title 8 prohibits dogs at large but accepts "visual and voice control," defined
as the owner being in the immediate presence of the animal and able to
demonstrate effective control. The one strict seasonal exception nearby is
Truckee River Regional Park, leashed May 1 through October 31.
