West River House does not allow pets. Its own terms and conditions put it in
one line: "Pets are not allowed. Bringing a pet on-site will incur a $200
charge, and the guest will be ejected without refund." The FAQ answers the pet
question with a single word. No.

Service animals are the published exception, and a procedural condition comes
attached: "If you are bringing a service animal, this must be acknowledged in
text or email by the time of registration." Registration happens before
arrival, so that acknowledgment does too.

The building is the one that operated as the Redlight until December 2024.
Same address, 10101 West River Street, one block south of Commercial Row and
the railroad tracks. The Redlight name is retired now; redlighttruckee.com
redirects to the West River House site, and the old speakeasy pages still
sitting on that domain are leftovers rather than current listings. The
current site doesn't mention a bar at all.

What it does describe is plain lodging. Bunk rooms with shared bathrooms, each
one a single sink, toilet, and shower in single-occupancy format. Private
rooms. A communal microwave and refrigerator in the entry room, which the site
is careful to say is not a full kitchen. There's no front desk. Booking and
check-in both happen on a phone before arrival, and the operators say they're
reachable by messaging.

Quiet hours run 9 PM to 9 AM in common spaces, 8 PM to 9 AM in the shared
dorms. Unregistered guests aren't permitted in private rooms, bunk rooms, or
common areas without prior approval. Smoking is off the property entirely, 25
feet from the building minimum, with a $500 cleaning fee for smoking or vaping
indoors.

Parking is the seasonal wrinkle. Downtown Truckee clears its streets in
winter, so on-street overnight parking disappears then, and the property points
to town parking roughly 150 feet from the entrance.

For Truckee lodging whose policy runs the other direction, Gravity Haus
Truckee-Tahoe publishes a dog-friendly page on its own site.
