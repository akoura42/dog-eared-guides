Old Town Tap doesn't publish a dog policy. Not on its website, not in its
Instagram or Facebook profiles. What's on the record is secondhand: a Yelp
thread where two users answer the dog question with the outdoor front patio
and an account labeled Business Owner replies that the restaurant has "limited
outdoor seating that is pet friendly," plus two dog-travel guides describing a
dog-friendly patio at this address. All of it points the same direction, and
only that far. Dogs outside, at the patio tables. The restaurant has confirmed
none of it in print, and no source says anything about the dining room.

The address is 10164 Donner Pass Road, Suite 1, in the storefront row running
through the middle of downtown. The patio the reports describe is the front
one, on the sidewalk along Donner Pass Road, which means the full downtown mix
a few feet away: pedestrians, cars hunting for parking, and freight trains on
the line behind the buildings. One of the travel guides counts five outdoor
tables. The restaurant publishes no count of its own.

Marlena and Luke bought the old Pizza Bar space and opened here in May 2016.
The kitchen runs Neapolitan-style pizza: margherita, mushroom, sausage and
kale, a meatball pie. Snacks include bone marrow and fried cheese curds.
Sixteen beer taps and twelve wine taps, and a spirits list that runs unusually
deep on mezcal and American whiskey. Dessert is Double 8 Dairy soft serve.

Truckee's town code accepts immediate voice and visual control in place of a
leash in most public places, but that's the town's rule and not this
restaurant's. Old Town Tap publishes nothing about leashes or water bowls. One
block over on Bridge Street, Alibi Ale Works states its own policy outright:
leashed dogs in the heated beer garden, never indoors. Hours here are noon to
9 Sunday through Thursday, noon to 10 Friday and Saturday.
