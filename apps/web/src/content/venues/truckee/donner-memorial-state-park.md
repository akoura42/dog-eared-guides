Dogs get most of Donner Memorial on a six-foot leash, and are shut out of four
specific places. State Parks publishes the line plainly: "Dogs are allowed on
the Lakeside Interpretive Trail, Zig Zag Trail, fire roads, and along the shore
of Donner Lake except for China Cove Beach." Off limits are the visitor center,
the half-mile Nature Trail, China Cove Beach, and designated ski trails under
snow. The six-foot rule is the park's own wording, and inside the park boundary
it replaces Truckee's town-wide voice-and-visual-control standard.

Two official documents word the allowed list differently. The park's October
2024 info sheet says "Lakeside Interpretive Trail, Splitrock Trail, fire roads,
campgrounds, and the shore of Donner Lake, except for China Cove Beach." Zig
Zag on the web page, Splitrock on the sheet; campgrounds named only on the
sheet. Both are State Parks publications, and neither retracts the other.

Winter narrows things further. Sierra District Order 683-22-002 bans dogs from
designated ski trails November 1 to May 1 when snow is present, and it names
the segments: the old route through the campground and day-use picnic areas,
the connector road from China Cove to the Splitrock loop, Creek campground to
Coldstream Road, and Emigrant Road along Cold Creek.

The shore is the draw. The same district order allows leashed dogs at East
Beach, on the eastern shore within 200 yards of the Tahoe-Donner boat launch.
China Cove, the swimming beach, is the one stretch that excludes them. Down at
the far end of the lake, West End Beach admits no dogs at all.

Mechanics. Day use runs $10 a vehicle, sunrise to sunset, seven days a week.
The visitor center opens 10 to 5 daily except Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New
Year's. The park sits on the south side of I-80 just west of downtown, at about
6,000 feet. Trail distances as posted: Nature Trail half a mile, China Cove
Fire Road seven-tenths, Lakeside Interpretive 1.4 miles, Splitrock two.

Nothing published mentions water for dogs, one way or the other.
