Truckee River Winery works out of a two-story red barn at 10061 South River
Street, about a quarter mile from the Amtrak depot downtown. The dog rule sits
in the winery's FAQ and runs one sentence: "Well-behaved dogs on leashes are
welcome in our outdoor garden and patio areas." Outdoors only. The limited
indoor seating among the barrels isn't covered by it.

A leash, specifically. Truckee's town code sets immediate voice and visual
control as the legal standard on streets and trails, so the winery's rule is
the stricter of the two, and it's the one that applies on this property.

There are chickens. The reservation page says so plainly: "we have roaming
chickens on the property." The FAQ asks guests to be mindful of other visitors
and the farm animals. This is a working production facility with a mini-farm
attached, not a patio behind a storefront.

The outdoor seating is small. Petite patios set into the garden, with fire pits
and blankets when it's cold. Nothing published mentions water bowls one way or
the other.

Tastings as posted: a three-varietal Fast Flight at $10, a four-varietal Barn
Tasting at $15, a five-varietal Signature at $25, and a $125 Tahoe Elevated
Winemaker Experience that includes a charcuterie board and a private tour of
the facility. Hours are Thursday through Sunday, 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. The winery
calls reservations highly recommended and takes walk-ins when there's room.
Capacity at the Truckee location tops out at 20 people, and groups over six go
through a large-group request form or the phone.

Outside food is allowed if it's self-contained and ready to eat. Outside
alcohol is not, which the FAQ attributes to licensing. Smoking and vaping are
prohibited on the property.

The winery started in 1989 in a two-car garage in Sierra Meadows and moved into
the Red Barn in 1996. Truckee River Regional Park lies upriver to the east, and
it's the town's one seasonal leash zone: leashes required there May 1 through
October 31.
