The Town of Truckee's animal code carves this park out by name, and the carve-out
splits the year in half. Section 8.01.420(d): "No person shall bring or allow any
dog or other animal in any area or portion of the Truckee River Regional Park
unless such animal at all times is kept on a leash and under full control of its
owner from May 1 to October 31." November 1 through April 30, the same subsection
drops back to the town's ordinary standard, the animal "in the immediate presence
and under the direct and effective control of the owner."

Truckee's voice-control rule holds nearly everywhere else in town. Here it's
suspended for six months.

Sixty-two acres, with about half a mile of uninterrupted river frontage,
according to the district's 2020–2030 master plan. Three agencies own pieces of
it: the rec district 23.57 acres, the sanitary district 18.55, the public utility
district 20.04. It reads as one park.

The Legacy Trail starts here. Paved, running east along the river toward
Glenshire as a segment of the Tahoe-Pyramid Trail, bikes allowed. The district's
trail page asks for leashes on it year-round: "Please keep your dogs on a leash
and pick up after them."

One posted exception to all of the above. Music in the Park, Wednesdays 6:30 to
8:30 p.m., June 17 through August 26, runs "No smoking and no dogs." Those
concerts fill the Salty Gebhardt Amphitheater lawn, which sits above the river at
the park's west side.

Parking is 99 striped spaces including five ADA, with dirt overflow the master
plan counts at roughly 483 total.

Construction runs through summer 2026 for the new Kidzone building. Disc golf
holes 11 and 12 are modified and the work zones are out of bounds. The rest of
the grounds hold ball fields, tennis courts, a skate park, a playground, a
community garden, and BBQ areas at the Lower River and Leisure sites.

The district lists the park at 10981 Truckee Way. Its own concert page gives the
address as 10500 Brockway Road. Park hours aren't posted online, and the number
above rings the district office rather than anything on site.
