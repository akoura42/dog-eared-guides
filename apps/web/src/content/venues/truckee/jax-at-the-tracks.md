The dog policy is two words on the restaurant's own site: "Dogs Allowed
Outside." It sits in the amenities list directly beside "Has Outdoor Seating,"
and that is the whole of what Jax publishes. Outside yes. Inside no.

The inside explains itself. Jax occupies a 1948 Kullman diner car that spent
its first decades in Pennsylvania as the Birmingham Grille, came west by truck,
and opened here on December 6, 1995 after a renovation the restaurant puts at
1.2 million dollars. Stainless steel and glass, a lunch counter, booths. A
diner car is narrow by construction, and the aisle between the counter and the
booths is narrow with it.

That leaves the outdoor tables. The site doesn't say how many there are, when
they get set out, or whether snow closes them for the season. Nothing about
water bowls one way or the other. No leash rule is published by the restaurant
either, which leaves the Town of Truckee's animal code as the operative one:
dogs at large are prohibited, but the Town accepts immediate voice and visual
control in place of a leash, defined as the owner being in the immediate
presence of the animal and able to demonstrate effective control of it.

The building sits on West River Street, between the Truckee River and the Union
Pacific main line. Trains pass close enough behind it that the name is literal
rather than decorative.

Posted hours run 7 a.m. to 8 p.m., every day. Breakfast comes off its own menu
until 2 p.m., dinner service starts at 4, and the lunch list covers the middle
of the day. The kitchen has been on Food Network, which the homepage links.

One third-party listing describes a dog menu here. The restaurant's own online
menu lists nothing of the kind.

The Amtrak depot is across the tracks at 10065 Donner Pass Road.
