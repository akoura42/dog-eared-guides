Alibi Ale Works puts the whole rule in one sentence on its own Truckee Public
House page: "Dogs are allowed on leash in our outdoor beer garden (weather
permitting) but not indoors." Leashed, outside, and only while the garden is
open.

That last clause carries weight in a town at this elevation. The beer garden is
heated, which the brewery says plainly, so it runs deep into the cold months.
But when weather does close it, there is no dog seating at all, because indoors
is off the table by the same sentence. Nothing is published about water bowls
one way or the other. No fee, no size limit, no breed limit appears anywhere on
the site.

The address is 10069 Bridge Street, one block off Commercial Row and the
railroad tracks that run through downtown. Alibi opened here in June 2017 as
its second location. The brewery itself is in Incline Village, Nevada; this
building is the public house and the live-music room.

Inside is communal seating. Minors are welcome. Outside is the large heated
garden, and it's the only part of the property a dog can be in.

Eighteen-plus Alibi beers rotate on draft, alongside cider, wine, and
non-alcoholic pours including two RationAle N/A beers. Cans, bottles, crowlers,
and growlers go to-go. The kitchen runs flatbreads, bao, a wagyu burger, a
crispy trout sandwich, poke and tofu bowls, and a beer-braised bratwurst, with
a seasonal rotation the brewery describes as leaning local.

Hours as posted: noon to 9 Monday through Thursday, noon to 10 Friday and
Saturday, noon to 9 Sunday. The kitchen closes an hour before the doors do.
Happy hour runs 3 to 5, Monday through Friday, off a short list of snacks and
six draft pours.
