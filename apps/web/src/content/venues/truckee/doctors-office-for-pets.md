"At Doctors Office for Pets, we see both cats and dogs." That line sits on the
clinic's homepage, and it is the whole of the published statement about who
gets seen here. Dogs come inside. They come in as patients, by appointment,
through the door at 10939A Industrial Way, Suite 101.

What the site doesn't publish is everything between the parking lot and the
exam room. No leash rule, no carrier rule, no waiting-room policy, nothing
about reactive dogs, nothing about drop-offs, no proof of vaccination required
to walk in. The FAQ page runs eight entries: hours, emergencies, spay and
neuter, surgery, appointment requests, vaccines, grain-free diets, heartworm
prevention. None of them addresses arrival. Truckee's animal ordinance governs
the street and the lot either way.

Third-party directory listings widen the species list to birds, reptiles,
pocket pets, and wildlife. The clinic's own pages claim none of that. Cats and
dogs is what it says.

Hours run Monday through Friday, eight to twelve-thirty, then one-thirty to
five, with the hour between closed for lunch. Saturday and Sunday, closed.
Appointments go through PetDesk or the phone.

The service list is long: wellness exams, preventative care, puppy and kitten
programs, vaccinations, spay and neuter, dentistry, surgery, x-rays, in-house
lab and bloodwork, abdominal ultrasound, laser therapy, hospitalization,
behavioral consultation, senior care. Daytime emergencies too. The clinic's
stated rule of thumb is that if something doesn't feel quite right, call, and
staff will help decide whether to come in. There is no overnight coverage.
After hours the site points to SVS Blue Pearl Emergency Hospital and Animal
Emergency and Specialty Center.

The address sits about three-quarters of a mile north of Commercial Row and
the railroad tracks. Doctors Office For Pets opened in 2006.
