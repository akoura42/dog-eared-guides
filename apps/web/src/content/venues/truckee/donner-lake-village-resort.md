Donner Lake Village doesn't take dogs, and it says so twice on its own site.
The FAQ answer is one line: "No, Donner Lake Village is not a pet-friendly
property." The terms and conditions reach further, because the restriction
isn't only the resort's: "NO PETS (including visitors who bring pets) are
allowed at any Donner Lake Village units or in the Donner Lake Village HOA
property and common areas." A dog arriving with a visiting friend falls under
the same sentence.

The consequence is published too. "Unauthorized occupancy of pets (including
visiting pets) will result in a fine of up to $250.00, immediate eviction, and
loss of all rent and fees." That figure is a fine, not a pet fee. There is no
rate at which a dog stays here.

Service dogs are the stated exception. Per the terms, they "are not considered
pets," and the mechanism is in-person: check in at the front desk, answer a few
questions, receive the dog regulations. Cleaning is billed on top at $75 an
hour if hair, food, stains, or damage turn up in the unit or the common areas.

Third-party listings blur all of this. Vrbo, Airbnb, and the directory sites
advertise pet-friendly rentals along the same stretch of Donner Pass Road under
names that read almost identically. Those are other buildings. The HOA language
covers this parcel and everything common on it.

The property sits at 15695 Donner Pass Road, on the north shore near the west
end of the lake, with its own beach and a seasonal dock. Check-in is 4 p.m. and
checkout is 11 a.m.; the FAQ says early arrivals and late departures aren't
permitted. Two saunas in Building B run first-come. Parking is capped at two
cars per unit.

The closest dog-accepting rooms in town are at Gravity Haus Truckee Tahoe, off
Highway 267 on the other side of downtown. For shoreline, the nearest public
beach to this end of the lake is West End Beach, which the recreation district
posts as no dogs. East Beach, about three miles east inside Donner Memorial
State Park, is the one stretch of Donner Lake shore where a leashed dog is
allowed by name.
