Gravity Haus Truckee-Tahoe is the hotel that was the Cedar House Sport Hotel,
at the same address on Brockway Road. Gravity Haus bought it in January 2022
and renamed it. The old domain redirects to the Gravity Haus property page now,
and the Cedar House Facebook account posted the change under its own name.

The dog policy sits on the property's FAQ, stated plainly: two dogs maximum per
room, $50 per dog per night, no fee for members. Dogs stay in the rooms rather
than in a kennel or a car. The same page asks that dogs be pre-approved by
phone before arrival, which puts a dog booking through the front desk instead
of straight through the reservation form.

The co-working space is the one other room the hotel names as dog-welcome:
conference room, work pods, open 7 a.m. to 10 p.m. Stella, the restaurant on
site, publishes nothing about dogs on its own website, and neither does the
hotel page. That silence covers both the dining room and the garden patios. The
hotel also doesn't publish a weight or breed limit, doesn't describe what a
dog-ready room actually contains, and posts no leash rule for the grounds.

Outside the room, the Town's rule is the operative one. Truckee prohibits dogs
at large but requires no leash. Voice and visual control means being in the
immediate presence of the animal and able to demonstrate effective control of
it.

The property is spread across several guest houses rather than one block, and
parking is free among them, with spots assigned at check-in. Check-in is 4 p.m.,
check-out 11 a.m. Hot tub from 9 a.m. to 10 p.m. and it takes a room key; the
sauna and gym open at 7 and don't.

Truckee River Regional Park is on the same road, at 10500 Brockway, under a
mile off. Dogs there have to be leashed from May 1 through October 31, the one
strict seasonal leash zone in town. The Truckee River Legacy Trail starts
inside that park and runs along the river toward Glenshire.
